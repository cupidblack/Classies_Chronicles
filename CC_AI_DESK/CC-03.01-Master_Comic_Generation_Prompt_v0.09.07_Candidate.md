# PART I — AUTHORITY, DEPENDENCIES AND STORY TRUTH

## 1. Operations, precedence, scope
Recognize `COMIC_REVIEW`, `COMIC_PLAN`, `COMIC_STORYBOARD`, `COMIC_GENERATE`, `COMIC_CONTINUE` (resume a genuinely partial run), `COMIC_REVISE` (same P/S IDs with a higher revision), `COMIC_ASSEMBLE`, `COMIC_VALIDATE`, and `COMIC_FINALIZE`. Review and planning do **not** implicitly generate art; generation does **not** authorize video, narration, repository modification, publishing, or a chronicle rewrite.

Instruction priority: law/platform and safety restrictions → owner/editor's explicit current commission and actual permissions → accepted canon, continuity and Public Output A → owner’s **latest explicit comic policy**, including this v0.09.07 candidate when selected → effective CC-01/02 requirements → specialist implementation → stylistic examples. If a higher-level effective document still says N16 or `SHEET_STEP`, register an **upstream alignment conflict**; do not silently claim it was edited. For the user’s current explicit proposed policy, use **N8 and `AUTO_COMPLETE`** in this specialist edition. Explicit N16 commissions still produce 16, within the limits. The historical Day 04/Day 05 N16 examples are not new defaults.

A request for more than 20 pages or fewer than 4 is outside this contract: report the limit and request authorization for a separate exceptional policy revision, or use an owner-authorized division into separate chronicle commissions. Never silently truncate. `COMIC_CONTINUE` resumes the existing commissioned N and its page cursor; it does not recalculate the default or re-render P01.

## 2. Single immutable eligible source and permissions
Lock `(season/chapter/episode/day, precise Public Output A title and date, public revision, source location and actual SHA-256 when accessible, exact public Challenge A, locked N, generation mode, original logos/reference provenance, cast appearance cards, P/H/F physical and informational states, prior episode's accepted outgoing state, rights/consent status, planned sheet/page IDs)`. Missing proofs are typed `UNVERIFIED` / `NOT_AVAILABLE` / `NOT_CHECKED`, not invented. A prior image is a continuity reference, not an authority overriding the source. Internal B1–B8, fifteen B6 handover divisions and nineteen existing CC-02.02 record families are read-only support, never inserted in the public comic. Restricted Challenge C answer/key is not used, displayed or exposed in generated art/prompts/metadata.

Unapproved character likenesses, corporate marks or third-party art do not acquire public-use rights merely because an image renderer can depict them. Provisional fictional drafts may be permissible within actual authorizations, but public release remains blocked without appropriate clearance. Show the Koware Group mark only where scene-justified and permitted; never merge it into the Classies title/identity.

## 3. Narrative truth firewall
Maintain distinct `P` present, `H` historical reference and `F` field/probe observation, plus `OBSERVED`, `REPORTED`, `CALCULATED`, `HYPOTHESIZED`, `FICTIONAL_EVENT`, `PENDING`, `UNVERIFIED` and `CONFIRMED` statuses. A local print/spool or transfer reference is not receipt by a custodian; a reported seal is not verified archive access; a conflicting wayfinding sign is not safe-route advice. # PART II — STYLE, CHARACTERS AND DAILY COVER-IN-STORY

## 4. Visual identity and continuity anchors
Create an **original cinematic, semi-photorealistic painted/ink graphic novel** with individualized stable Ghanaian and other source-authorized characters, believable anatomy and hand-object contact, purposeful cinematic angles, navy/teal restrained interface colors, warm gold legible caption boxes, white high-contrast balloons and consistent panel dividers. Maintain distinct actor face, hairstyle, body build, clothing, expressions, carried props, scene geography and light trajectory across every sheet. A small temporary comic exaggeration is acceptable only for an actual harmless comedic beat; never drift into a different franchise style or permanently off-model caricature.

The chronicle’s appropriate approved Classies Chronicles graphic/logo may appear in P01’s header; if no exact cleared asset is available, use simple original typeset `CLASSIES CHRONICLES` rather than inventing a false official emblem. A Koware Group corporate mark remains separately scene-dependent. Do not add a decorative logo as a clue or historical proof.

## 5. Binding P01 hybrid-cover rule (NEW, v0.09.07)
**P01 remains P01: it must visibly cover the chronicle’s very first actual scene, act or beat, not become a standalone poster, title-only page, synthetic prologue or unused spacer.** Dedicate approximately the **upper 25%–33% of the *complete 9:16 P01 canvas*** to a readable, attractive **introductory header**. This is an art-direction target, not a crop mandate: the opening action may begin inside or behind the header and continue seamlessly below it, with contrast/safe-area protection so the letters remain readable.

The P01 header must identify: (a) `CLASSIES CHRONICLES` as the main franchise mark/text, (b) the **exact daily chronicle title**, (c) its true **chronicle date** and (d) season/chapter/episode/day identifier in a subordinate compact line as space permits. The episode title may be included in a subordinate hierarchy, **never replacing the daily title**. Spellcheck against the chosen Output A. The larger graphic may symbolically reuse an **actual object, document, door, clock, landmark, relevant actor action or environmental feature from the *first scene***, with a restrained composition. It may foreshadow themes already visible in the opening beat, but must not spoil an unseen revelation or suggest unverified facts.

**Panel construction:** define a `P01_header_region` inside `P01_canvas`, with desired approximate fraction `[0.25, 0.33]` measured from the top. The first genuine action occupies some portion of this region or begins at/under its boundary, followed by enough space below for sequential panels/dialogue. Suggested format: **integrated illustrated masthead/establishing first panel → 1–3 additional chronological panels**. A minimum of one clearly advancing event/action from the source must occur on P01; the scene must not be delayed until P02. For readability, allow visual transitions/overlays rather than hard split or force-fit multiple balloons into the header. Where precise text cannot be generated reliably, render clean art-safe areas and deterministically letter the verified title/date after illustration.

**Do not:** consume a page number for a separate cover; repeat the whole header or daily title on P02…PN; put a cover-only quadrant in S01; repeat an opening montage on each later sheet; insert Day 04/05 or episode title from a prior run; print an internal QA or Challenge C key; use the introductory header as an unsolicited second challenge. Later pages may use a short small consistent series/page label, never a new full cover. If a user explicitly requests an independent promotional cover **as an additional deliverable**, it is distinct and **does not count toward N**.

## 6. Practical first-page and cross-page continuity
Record P01 header copy, symbol/source rationale, top-fraction estimate, first action/first panel and last action, initial-to-outgoing cast and props, title-safe zones, distinct page purpose and first-scene source paragraph. At each subsequent P, import the preceding *accepted for working continuity* art/reference plus outgoing-state capsule. Treat this as internal workflow acceptance only, not owner publication approval.

# PART III — PAGE BUDGET, BATCH GEOMETRY AND CHALLENGE

## 7. Page budget (NEW, v0.09.07)
For an ordinary daily chronicle select **N=8**. Allowed integer range is **4 ≤ N ≤ 20**. The user’s explicit count within the range wins; otherwise retain N8 even for a long-looking manuscript unless a documented editorial page-budget decision recommends another eligible N and the owner has authorized automatic count selection. Do not silently increase N to 16. Do not silently reduce an explicitly commissioned N16 to the new default N8. N counts all story-bearing pages **including the integrated P01 cover-story page** and the final challenge-bearing PN.

Editorial reference, not an automatic quota: short → 4 pages when specifically chosen; ordinary → 8; lengthy → 12–16 when specifically chosen; very long → up to 20 when specifically chosen. No filler, duplicate beats or artificial four-page padding. Make one immutable map of unique source-backed `P01…PN` purposes before any render. Multiple pages may dramatize one scene only if each advances new causal events; preserve first-to-last source order except legitimate source-grounded parallel cutting.

**Whole and remainder handling:** let `q=floor(N/4)`, `r=N mod 4`. Render **q complete zero-gutter 2×2 sheets** of four genuine pages. For `r=1,2,3`, render precisely those **r separate full 9:16 pages** (not filler or extra collages); a delivery gallery/package may contain them but each keeps its own complete page file. The final Challenge A always goes at the bottom of **PN**, including a leftover single page. For N4: S01[P01–P04]; N8: S01[P01–P04], S02[P05–P08]; N20: five sheets ending P20. N7: S01[P01–P04] + separate P05, P06, P07. All page IDs are unique and sequential.

## 8. Exact aspect-ratio and genuine native-detail contract (v0.09.06 retained)
Every delivered complete page, including P01 with header, passes exact **9:16** as integer pixels: `16 × width == 9 × height`. Target each page at **genuine** 1080×1920 native art or higher exact 9:16 where the provider supports it; a pixel-resampled export cannot be relabeled “native.” For a zero-gutter 2×2 group of true 1080×1920 pages, output sheet is 2160×3840. More generally, for `c` columns × `r` rows of complete 9:16 pages, `sheet_ratio=(9c):(16r)` before gutters. A common 1024×1536 sheet yields four **512×768 (2:3)** cells and is **noncompliant**, even if the prompts said “portrait.” A 1080×1920 *sheet* would have 540×960 per page, aspect-compliant but low native pixel detail.

**Provider capability preflight:** verify from a real provider setting or measured trial whether native sheet render yields genuine 9:16 internal page cells with adequate text quality. If not, render **four separately generated, genuine 9:16 pages** and assemble losslessly to a 2×2 image; maintain separate page files. If provider cannot return real 9:16 art in either mode, mark `C11_PAGE_ASPECT=FAIL/BLOCKED`, preserve original art as reference-only, and **stop rather than repeatedly produce invalid geometry**. Composition-aware redraw/outpainting/re-layout is permissible only with real provenance and inspection. Do not stretch faces, truncate balloons, fake compliance with blank side padding or convert low-resolution upscale into a native-quality claim.

Include page borders, introductory P01 header and any text safe regions *inside* the page canvas. Measure original raster, sheet/cell coordinates, every extracted/assembled page, post-lettering/export raster and SHA-256 for files truly read. A nominal requested image size is never final proof. Strict ratio has **no percentage tolerance**. Other QA such as legibility, consent and scene fidelity remains independently necessary even when C11 passes.

## 9. Panels, typography, lettered evidence
Every P is an internally readable graphic-novel **page** (normally 2–4 scene panels; P01 can integrate its top illustrated header/panel), not one image per act mislabeled as a whole page. Within each P use conventional English reading order. In 2×2 sheets scan TL→TR→BL→BR; never let balloons or characters span page-cell boundaries. Keep text phone-readable in actual extracted 9:16 view with reasonable top/bottom UI protection; suggested large-letter starting values at true 1080-width are testing targets, not guaranteed universal sizes. For critical names, dates, authorization stamps, signs, timestamps, Scripture citations and Challenge A, prefer deterministic typography over generative text guesses; verify the **final composited raster**. Correct misleading background text rather than covering it with a new balloon.

## 10. Only the global final challenge
Store exact authorized **public Challenge A** from Public Output A in a controlled run packet, but send it to the image/lettering step **only for PN**. All P01…P(N−1), including the P01 daily header and the fourth page of each nonfinal sheet, have `challenge_allowed=false`. Exactly one complete Challenge A appears **at the literal bottom of PN**, with nothing after it; no intermediate “Classies Challenge,” answer hint, lesson-summary, end card or repeated teaser. Never access or reveal restricted Challenge C material. If the last page is a remainder standalone P, the same placement still applies.

# PART IV — CONTINUOUS GENERATION AND RESUMABLE STATE

## 11. Mode routing (NEW, v0.09.07)
**Default: `AUTO_COMPLETE`.** An explicit request to implement the Master Comic Generation Prompt for a selected daily chronicle authorizes generation of its **full N-page sequence in the active response/task**, even though the renderer may need multiple sequential calls. Do not insert an owner-input gate after S01/S02, and **do not wait for repeated “continue” messages**. Between batches, perform automatic nonpublic mechanical and story-continuity checks; advance only if they pass, marking `INTERNAL_WORKING_QA_PASS`, **not** `OWNER_APPROVED` or `RELEASE_APPROVED`.

**Opt-in:** `SHEET_STEP` only if user explicitly requests “one collage at a time,” “wait after each batch,” “pause for approval,” or equivalent. It emits one sheet and stops. `PAGE_STEP` only if user explicitly asks for one page per turn. An explicit user pause, stop or subset `[Pnn–Pmm]` also limits that call. A user's permission to continue generation **does not** waive safety, tool constraints, missing authoritative source, geometry checks or content/rights limits. Do not treat a general request to generate comics as `SHEET_STEP` merely because v0.09.05 used it.

**Feasibility:** `AUTO_COMPLETE` means a best-effort continuous **synchronous** sequence, not a background job, future scheduled delivery or guarantee that every provider can return unlimited images. If a generation platform/tool cannot be called again in the active interaction, or a critical source/geometry/rights problem prevents faithful rendering, truthfully report the completed page IDs, current blocker, and a precise resume cursor; do not promise silent completion later. If all images were generated but QA failed, label the artifact provisional and disclose repairs needed. Do not create misleading accepted checks.

## 12. Uninterrupted production state machine
`COMMISSION_VALIDATED → SOURCE_LOCKED → N_STORYBOARD_LOCKED → FIRST_PAGE_HYBRID_COVER_BRIEF_LOCKED → PROVIDER_GEOMETRY_PREFLIGHT → BATCH_READY → RENDER_DRAFT → MEASURE/EXTRACT → CHECK_C01–C22_APPLICABLE_SUBTESTS → [INTERNAL_WORKING_QA_PASS → CHECKPOINT → NEXT_BATCH] OR [REPAIR_SAME_IDS → RECHECK] OR [BLOCKED_STOP] → PN_FINAL_CHALLENGE_QA → GLOBAL_QA → COMIC_FINALIZE`.

1. **Preflight once:** verify eligible complete source, selected N, mode, rights/canon/clock states, exact public Challenge A, P01 header/data, all P01…PN unique purposes, visual references and capability to render real 9:16. Freeze an N-page storyboard before the first image.
2. **Batch exact next IDs:** q full sheets of four, then r individual leftovers. For S01 brief P01 as the hybrid cover **with first-scene action**, not separate graphic. Later batches receive only their current page briefs plus short continuity anchors, **never the global synopsis to restart the story**.
3. **Each batch image call:** require correct IDs, row-major order, real page boundaries, 9:16 pixels, character likeness, environment, typography, source-truth and challenge settings. This is one batch in a multi-batch authorized run; generating the next batch need not involve a user prompt in `AUTO_COMPLETE`.
4. **Before next batch:** inspect actual raster/crops; validate titles/P01 fraction and scene advancement when applicable; check no challenge until PN; record hashes, transitions, revisions and QA statuses. If serious fault, attempt a reasonable **bounded same-ID repair** when feasible; if unresolved, hold cursor, stop and report, not produce faulty downstream batches.
5. **Checkpoint:** save last panel's current place/time, actor poses, outfits, held objects, unresolved questions, visible evidence state, and actual image reference. In `AUTO_COMPLETE`, checkpoint is internal and **does not wait for owner acceptance**; at the end request or record any needed final editorial approval. In stepped mode checkpoint and stop as requested.
6. **Final:** check total unique pages=N, P01 hybrid cover/story, Challenge A only at bottom PN, exact pixel geometry, native/detail provenance, full C01–C22 results, rights/source and persistence. Deliver a consolidated honest report, never call the comic public-ready merely because N images exist.

## 13. Render prompt compiler
For each call build five blocks: **A visual/style lock**, **B page IDs and geometry**, **C distinct page action/lettering**, **D incoming/outgoing checkpoint, explicit exclusions**, **E final-page challenge switch**. Input only authorized current pages and short reference images/look cards. Do not stuff the whole episode, old rejected images or all future page dialogues into every image request. For S01, add an explicit P01 integrated-header block; for subsequent batches turn it **off**.

**Binding P01 addition:** “Inside complete 9:16 comic P01 only, creatively integrate the Classies Chronicles daily-chronicle header across approximately the upper quarter to third of P01. Accurately letter its daily title, date and season/chapter/episode/day ID. Incorporate a genuine physical element from the first scene into the masthead and show the scene’s opening action progressing *on P01 itself* beneath/through the header. Not a standalone cover page, not a repeated logo/splash; P02–PN continue without this large header.”

**Binding geometry addition:** “Make only authorized current complete page IDs. Each page must be **measured exact 9:16**, including P01 header. If four-page composite, use row-major 2×2 cells of genuine 9:16 pages, with total zero-gutter sheet proportional to 2160×3840 for native 1080×1920 pages. No 1024×1536 2:3 sheet/512×768 cells pretending compliance. If sheet-native unsupported, render exact individual 9:16 pages and assemble losslessly, verifying all cells. No stretching or label clipping. Renderer requests cannot replace actual pixel QA.”

**Binding continuation addition:** “This is batch `Sxx`, pages `[IDs]`, part of a locked N-page `AUTO_COMPLETE` run. Use the actual prior accepted-working visual checkpoint. Do not re-cover, recap, duplicate beats, invent outcomes or prematurely end/challenge the story. After internal geometry/continuity QA, **proceed directly with the next batch in this same active operation** unless a genuine blocker or explicitly selected stepping mode requires a stop.”

**Challenge switch:** if current IDs exclude PN, pass `PUBLIC_CHALLENGE_ALLOWED=false` and **omit Challenge A text**; if PN is included, allow exact Challenge A **only in PN’s bottommost reserved safe zone**. No challenge elsewhere.

## 14. Revisions and exceptions
Preserve immutable source-art snapshots. Repair `P07` as `P07-r02`, not `P17`; repair `S02` as `S02-r02`, not a replacement story. Native source, assembled page, outpainted page, upscaled export and final lettered page are distinct provenance records. Record both file identity and real measured dimensions. Distinguish style reference acceptance, pixel geometry, internal continuity pass, rights, owner editorial acceptance, release and publication.

If user expressly opts for a nonconforming draft, status is **`GEOMETRY_EXCEPTION_DRAFT_ONLY`**, never `C11_PAGE_ASPECT=PASS` or publication approval. An unsupported provider or blocked rights/source ends `AUTO_COMPLETE` at the last valid checkpoint with an honest report. A re-run on an old N16 commission preserves N16; do not silently restart it at default N8.

## 15. Deliverables and cadence
During generation share visual batches as generated if UI/tool requires; **do not require a new user message between successful batches in `AUTO_COMPLETE`**. Hold verbose QA notes internally where file access allows. At successful final PN, give the intended finished images/page files or real links, one consolidated QA/manifest, remaining approval/rights caveats and, if applicable, a resume or repair inventory. In `SHEET_STEP` / `PAGE_STEP` provide the explicitly requested single deliverable then stop. No implicit cloud persistence, future asynchronous completion, GitHub commit, public release or CC-04 media generation.
