# PART I — PURPOSE, OPERATIONS AND AUTHORITIES

## 1. Literary identity and creative purpose

Classies Chronicles is an original ongoing serial about Koware Group, its people, relationships, members, business and community life; Enka and his special identity Cupid Black; Blue Crown R&D and the experimental Edubeam; the Eternal Dreamfall study tradition; the role-driven work and dilemmas of staff; and journeys through historical comparison, contemporary life and speculative futures. The writing may combine action, mystery, humane slapstick, humor, romance, philosophy, faith, education, suspense, travel, investigative journalism and science-fiction consequences. Its dramatic center is **a question, desire or conflict, opposition, consequential choices, an earned outcome, and meaningful continuity**.

Depict real human texture—commuting, work deadlines, food, language, money, neighbors, equipment, illness and safety, misunderstandings, loyalty, celebrations and setbacks—without demeaning roles or making every character a mouthpiece for corporate announcements. A role spotlight and deployed field team usually carry perspective; Enka does not automatically lead every episode. Faith, news, safety and business context should affect **what people do and what happens next**, not become detached lessons. Keep fictional narration immersive; perform reality/permission labeling in internal records, not as repeated intrusions into story prose. The story is original and must not copy the distinctive expression of a third-party science-fiction or comics franchise.

## 7. Calendar authority and exact date-to-episode algorithm

Use the owner-approved production calendar and timezone where established. A Phase/Probe API may help validate proposed chapter boundaries but is not unilateral authority to change a separately approved schedule. Accept an explicit approved phase-start civil date and phase-end convention; convert offset-aware timestamps to the approved production timezone *before* date arithmetic. Determine the first **Monday on or after** the local phase-start date as a candidate chapter anchor. For each complete 14-calendar-day block beginning at that anchor, the ten eligible Monday–Friday dates map consecutively to Chronicle #1–#10 and chapter working days 1–40 across up to four blocks. **Confirm that the whole block fits the authorized chapter boundary**, not merely the requested target date. A leftover partial block does not become a normal complete episode without an approved exception.

Deterministic validation algorithm:

1. Resolve target **production civil date** in the approved IANA timezone; record separately the retrieval timestamp UTC and any historical requested-date basis.
2. Resolve effective chapter ID, local start, local end and whether the supplied end is inclusive or exclusive. If no convention is approved, flag ambiguity before assigning a definitive identity.
3. Compute `anchor_date = first Monday on or after chapter_start_date` unless an approved exception supplies another anchor.
4. Calculate whole-day `offset = target_local_date − anchor_date`; do not use elapsed hours (DST and midnight errors matter). Reject negative offsets and target dates outside effective chapter scope.
5. Compute `block = floor(offset / 14)`, `block_day = offset mod 14`; ordinary episode index is `block + 1` only for `block` in 0–3 and an approved fully fitting two-week block.
6. Accept only `block_day` in `{0,1,2,3,4,7,8,9,10,11}` corresponding to weekdays Monday–Friday. Reject or specially route Saturdays, Sundays, nonchapter periods and invalid partial periods.
7. Map `block_day` `{0,1,2,3,4}` to Chronicles `1–5` and `{7,8,9,10,11}` to `6–10`; set `chapter_workday = 10×block + chronicle_slot`.
8. Cross-check the weekday, approved episode schedule, role-day records, duplicate/accepted prior installment and known archive/legacy ID before writing. A mismatch causes `BLOCKED` or a clearly documented provisional schedule, never an invented accepted identity.

Boundary regression examples (arithmetic fixtures, not claims about a live chapter): chapter working days 1–5 = episode 1/Chronicles #1–#5; 6–10 = episode 1/#6–#10; 11 = episode 2/#1; 15 = episode 2/#5; 20 = episode 2/#10; 21 = episode 3/#1; 30 = episode 3/#10; 31 = episode 4/#1; 40 = episode 4/#10. A phase beginning Wednesday does not make Wednesday Chronicle #1. A production-day reassignment, public holiday schedule, weekend special or archival compression requires an actual exception record.

# PART IV — ETERNAL DREAMFALL RESEARCH AND CUMULATIVE STORY MERGE

## 16. Bible study: a seven-year cycle and five-year material allocation

Treat the Eternal Dreamfall Bible study as an actual sourced calendar, not an automatically dated literary theme. The **proposed cycle** began in 2023 and lasts seven calendar years: two preparation/research years followed by five reading years. Ordinary reading begins with the first complete Sunday–Saturday week of the third year, **Sunday 5 January 2025** in the 2023 cycle. A complete five-year period has approximately 260 Sunday–Saturday weeks, while the source list has **250 named Week entries**; the two numbers are different, and no agent may invent ten missing readings or equate an arbitrary week label with a daily passage.

For an authorized production year Y, proposed recurring-cycle arithmetic is `cycle_start = 2023 + 7×floor((Y−2023)/7)`. Reading-year eligibility depends on that cycle's two preliminary years. Calculate `reading_week = floor((target_local_date − first_full_reading_sunday) / 7) + 1`, using the **correctly authorized study-calendar timezone**. First verify whether the owner has a separate approved week-to-entry and day-to-passage allocation; use that over any mechanical scheme. If no approved allocation is available but a provisional run is specifically authorized, apply the explicitly labeled proposal `PROVISIONAL_ENTRY_EQUALS_WEEK` for weeks 1–250; designate weeks 251–260 only for review/research/consolidation rather than inventing extra listed readings. A weekly entry does **not** define seven specific daily assignments; derive a transparent internal `GENERATED_DAILY_STUDY_PLAN` across Sunday–Saturday, mindful of literary units, passage lengths and obligations. ## 19. Eight-operation semantic merge loop

The governing Context Merge Engine asks for **semantic assimilation**, not concatenation. 1. **READ:** inspect the actual new source, date, permission and factual/fictional classification.
2. **DECOMPOSE:** extract characters, facts, events, conflicts, motifs, tension, choices, chronology and risks.
3. **LOCK:** classify existing narrative anchors as `HARD`, `SOFT` or `OPTIONAL`; source facts remain separately locked to their evidence.
4. **CONNECT:** choose a causal, thematic, relational, environmental, historical or visual bridge into existing story events.
5. **TRANSFORM:** rewrite the affected scenes and, when necessary, other transitions so the new meaning lives inside the plot.
6. **PRESERVE:** test that all indispensable hard anchors, relationships, relative event order, source intentions and essential earlier payoffs survive.
7. **VALIDATE:** check factual provenance, canon, clock, physical position, realism, rights, scope and narrative coherence; reject unsupported material.
8. **REGISTER:** document source IDs, pre/post scene, retained anchors, new motif, bridge type, changed choice/consequence, QA, exclusions and editorial status.
