## A. Proposed replacement for ordinary comics count in CC-01.01 §42

> **Default eight-page daily mobile comic.** When a Classies Chronicles daily story receives a separate comic commission with no authorized page count and the selected effective specialist is `CC-03.01 / CC-MCGP v0.09.08` or an explicitly adopted compatible successor, produce **N=8 complete distinct 9:16 portrait story pages** in **two** exact 2×2 four-page sheets. A **very short daily chronicle may instead use N=4**, with a documented editorial determination that four unique source-supported page beats suffice while preserving the opening scene, development, outcome and final exact public Challenge A. Four is the minimum. An expressly commissioned N=12, 16, 20 or any other authorized positive multiple of four overrides the default. Do not silently shorten an earlier N16 commission.
>
> **Four-page-native-geometry obligation.** Every sheet consists of **exactly four different complete comic pages**, TL/TR/BL/BR, and **every individual page** must be exact 9:16 (`16×W=9×H`) with a **minimum of 1080×1920 source/detail-bearing pixels**. Therefore the normal minimum two-by-two sheet is **2160×3840** pixels. A 1024×1536 sheet is 2:3 and yields 512×768 2:3 quadrants; a 1080×1920 sheet has correct ratio but yields 540×960 under-resolution quadrants. Neither is acceptable. Do not certify an enlarged/letterboxed/cropped image as native compliant. If a renderer cannot actually deliver the required four pages in one high-detail sheet, render four individually compliant pages and compose the exact sheet mechanically with no rescaling; otherwise record a capability blocker rather than inventing a PASS.
>
> **Separate covers and story-bearing introductions.** Produce one appropriately approved standalone full-bleed 9:16 season cover for the whole season and one corresponding chapter cover for the whole chapter when those collections are commissioned. They are **not** part of daily N and do not consume a 2×2 story quadrant. Instead of wasting a story page on a separate episode/day title splash, make the **first real comic page P01** introduce its episode in the upper **1/4–1/3** or each daily chronicle in the upper **1/5–1/4** while actively depicting the actual first scene/act below or blended into the header. If P01 opens the episode and first daily chronicle simultaneously, use **one 1/4-height header**, not two. Display the **single chosen Classies Chronicles logo once**, episode title, correct season/chapter/episode and where applicable Chronicle #NN; do not redundantly type the franchise name beside its full logo. Small optional folios elsewhere may take the form `Season 00 - Chapter 05 - Episode 03 - Chronicle #05  01/08`. Do not repeat oversized introduction branding on later story pages. Ensure the full-page season/chapter covers and integrated openings respect approved rights and titles; keep cover artwork uncluttered.
>
> **Challenge, source, count and release.** The public Challenge A appears **exactly once**, at the literal bottom of global story page PN of the separately commissioned story sequence; nothing follows. Preserve one immutable public source Output A, canonical character and P/H/F continuity, accepted rights/consent, original C01–C22 identifiers, honest file/pixel/provider provenance, actual quality checks and separate owner publication authorization. In sheet-only mode N must be divisible by four; resolve incompatible N without filler pages. One `SHEET_STEP` produces the next four unique P IDs only and stops pending a new user continuation.

Keep all remaining CC-01.01 §42 rules compatible with this targeted replacement, particularly rights, genuine image inspection, lettering legibility, human QA, actual file evidence, and step-gated continuity.

## B. Proposed update for CC-01.01 §46 `C18_COUNT_INVENTORY`

> `C18_COUNT_INVENTORY`: Confirm exactly the expressly authorized **N** unique source-supported complete story pages, numbered P01…PN, and (for exact four-page sheet delivery) exactly **N/4** unique 2×2 sheet assets with four ordered and separately measurable page crops. Default daily **N8 → two sheets**; authorized very-short **N4 → one sheet**; explicitly extended **N16 → four sheets**. Season and chapter full-page covers are **separately counted collection assets** and the P01 episode/daily introductions are **inside** story P01, never added to N. Maintain repair revision history and real output evidence; a sheet image by itself does not prove separate page exports or true native per-cell resolution.

Leave all C01–C22 identifiers unchanged. Treat new cover, header and native-minimum checks as internal subtests of C01/C04/C10/C11/C12/C13/C14/C18/C19/C20/C21, **not** C23 or any renamed gate. Other existing C-gate criteria remain active.

## C. Proposed sweep of unqualified master references

Where CC-01.01 §52, §42, commission examples or routing text call an unqualified daily comic a “standard sixteen-page” or “four-sheet standard,” revise to **“standard eight-page daily comic in two 2×2 sheets; four-page short exception in one sheet; longer explicit N permitted.”** Do **not** change any unrelated media/video shot count, clip duration, story chapter structure, archival/continuity format or season schedule. A comic page is not the same unit as a video act or shot. Add `SEASON_COVER` and `CHAPTER_COVER` as explicit comic operations within CC-01.01's comic router **only after adoption**, distinct from `COMIC_GENERATE` and `COMIC_CONTINUE`.

## D. Versioned adoption checklist

1. Owner/editor approves exact N8/N4 and cover/header requirements, records approval date, source revision and decision ID.
2. Issue a **new separately versioned** CC-01.01 master; do not overwrite unapproved historical text without governance.
3. Select `CC-03.01`, `03.02`, `03.03 v0.09.08` for pilot scope, preserving existing explicit commissioned N values.
4. Approve an exact logo asset/version and necessary rights; establish provisional vs final season/chapter title states.
5. Validate real native/detail-bearing ≥1080×1920 pages and four-cell sheets at ≥2160×3840; verify source text/folio and one-logo integrated first page.
6. Run actual N4 and N8 pilot checks with the official C01–C22 gate matrix and new SC098 subtests.
7. Only then consider publication. Documentary patch preparation is not deployment or GitHub persistence.
