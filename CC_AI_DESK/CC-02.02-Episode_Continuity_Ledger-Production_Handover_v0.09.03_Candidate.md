## 1. Purpose, ownership and nonnegotiable invariants

An ordinary episode encompasses **up to ten separately commissioned weekday chronicles in two ordinary Monday–Friday weeks**. Approved calendar exceptions are explicit and must not fabricate missing days. Each episode archive owns its approved manifest, accepted story revisions, provenance records, participant states, day events, challenge records and protected handover. `CC_AI_DESK` holds reusable prompt, role/canon definitions and catalogs, not a default warehouse for sensitive episode diary bodies or changing field-mission records. Chapter/season/series rollups cite accepted episode heads rather than overwriting them.

**Invariants to enforce:**

1. A single verifiable **accepted head** per scoped episode revision at a time; parallel draft branches may exist but never both claim the same accepted revision without reconciliation.
2. The accepted projection includes only effective authenticated accepted events. `PROPOSED` narrative changes remain hypothetical even if written beautifully and even if a downloadable file exists.
3. Every substantive state change retains causal source A revision and scene, pre-state, effect, correction/supersession lineage, owner decision and actual effective order.
4. Production date P, historical reference H, Probe F and the **physical position of each person** are separate dimensions. No unproved teleportation, automatic retrieval, memory, recovery or future-news claim.
5. A real-world Koware task status is sourced externally; a fictional event cannot change a factual business register by inference.
6. Privacy, copyright and personal consent are purpose- and audience-scoped. Restricted keys, diary bodies, credentials, private addresses and sensitive identity evidence do not enter public A or routine B or ordinary transfer ZIPs.
7. Public A ends with one answer-free final Challenge A; internal B1–B8 is **not appended** after it in the same public artifact. B6 carries no solution, revealing near-hint or private key rationale.
8. Neither `FULL_DERIVATION_REVIEW` nor a daily run self-commissions comics, videos, season rollups, prizes, publication, external writes or canon approval.
9. Derivatives retain exact source revision and rights dependencies; changed A/challenge/consent/canon sets affected assets `STALE_SOURCE` until revalidated.
10. A new document edition is not a deployed schema migration; old approved URLs, story IDs, legacy `calendar_probe_record`, old B3/B7 meanings and past decisions remain traceable.
11. An absent record is **not** proof of a zero event; `NO_CHANGE` requires affirmative verification, and an inaccessible source is not fictitiously marked “checked.”
12. Lawful privacy deletion, revocation and redaction by authorized custodian prevail over simplistic append-forever rules; preserve audit evidence only to the extent legally authorized.

## 2. Audience isolation, access and safe export

| Class | Typical data | Authorized storage/transport | Public exposure |
|---|---|---|---|
| `PUBLIC` | **Approved** audience-facing manuscript A, its complete public Challenge A, rights-cleared credit, approved media | Human-authorized publication target | Only with independent public release authorization |
| `INTERNAL` | Unreviewed draft A, protected B1–B8, source/research assessment, authorized role chart snapshots, approvals metadata, event graph and handover | Approved private episode/editor workspace, permission-filtered providers | Never silently public just because a file URL exists |
| `RESTRICTED` | Challenge answer/rationale/variants and key material; private diaries; sensitive consent proofs; personal identifiers, secrets and credentials | Actual purpose-specific, access-controlled secure custody | Not via ordinary daily chat, public repo, generic B, shared ZIP or image/video metadata |

**Access is not use authorization.** The following are separate choices that must have their own evidence: reading; internal processing; generating fictional portrayal; quoting; creating likeness, voice or media; publishing; exporting to another AI/platform; retaining; granting onward rights; rewarding or processing participants; and responding to revocation. A user can authorize a provisional *private draft* without providing public-person consent, third-party material licensing, or competitive challenge custody.

`challenge_answer_key` is a **schema family**, not an automatic output. It is instantiated only on an independent restricted commission after exact authorized recipient, genuine secure storage, access control, key owner and operating/reveal rules have been verified. Ordinary B may carry a nonrevealing `key_custody_status` such as `NOT_CREATED` or an opaque authorized reference that reveals neither answer nor reasoning. If no secure store is available, record `RESTRICTED OUTPUT NOT CREATED; HUMAN SECURE CUSTODY REQUIRED` and keep competition/reveal pending.

**Portable-transfer filter:** strip private diary bodies, legal proof images, home addresses, employee record contents, answer keys, credentials, unrelated contacts and unlicensed media from shared handovers; replace with rights-safe statuses, provenance class, opaque controlled ID and custodian request route where permissible. If no safe B recipient exists, retain the complete internal packet as `RESTRICTED_NOT_EMITTED` and transmit only a sanitized subset rather than leaking by convenience.

## 5. Calendar, clock, source and role snapshot contracts

An episode context record must preserve **the approved effective local timezone**, phase start/end and inclusive/exclusive semantics, `anchor` (first Monday on/after local phase start), target local civil date, `offset_days` as whole civil days, `block = floor(offset_days/14)`, `block_day`, accepted episode 1–4, slot 1–10, chapter workday 1–40, full episode fit and exception. Weekends `{5,6,12,13}` in a 14-day block are not ordinary chronicles. The actual approved ID/legacy map outranks algorithmically proposed folder paths. A speculative Probe phase never supplies chapter authority.

For each of P/H/F preserve the original source payload/field, URL, retrieval UTC, observed-as-of, offset-aware ISO when present, parser and HTTP status, approved timezone conversion, source confidence, source freshness and **independently assigned place**. Requested story date P on a retrospective run is not today's date unless explicitly requested; H is an historical reference not an auto-transport; F is a mission/probe temporal scenario, not verified future news. Raw user-supplied fallback samples remain dated old observations, not live.

Maintain person-centric accepted `location_state` covering character, time coordinate, physical location, modality, travel source event, latest contact, last knowledge, uncertainty and reason. Also retain HQ/lab/site baselines only where actually approved and non-sensitive. Device movement must be compatible with sequential person transfers, energy and era canon. A false claim that “everyone is back at HQ” would violate mission and character state even if the prose is convenient.

A `research_register` must preserve independent actual **event**, **publication**, and **retrieval** dates, a recognizable authority class, claim/corroboration status, source permission and whether the fact was used, paraphrased or rejected. Study allocation may be `APPROVED` or a specifically authorized `PROVISIONAL_ENTRY_EQUALS_WEEK`; a 250-entry weekly source is not seven automatically discovered daily passages. Verse provider/date and HS100/HS1000 lesson distinctions must remain discoverable, not flattened into a fabricated “daily course completed.”

## 6. Continuity-event lifecycle and accepted-state projection

### 6.1 Event body — mandatory meaning

An event must identify: immutable actual `event_id`; episode/day scope and exact original A revision; scene/beat; narrated fictional occurrence time and *physical* location; actor/participants; affected characters, artifacts, missions and arcs; real-vs-fictional type; accepted pre-state references; causal prerequisites and required knowledge; proposed specific delta and conditional post-state; technology/era and rights checks; lifecycle `PROPOSED`, `ACCEPTED`, `REJECTED`, `SUPERSEDED`; decision/approver/effective time where present; and correction/reversal/supersession references. When actual fields are unavailable, preserve absence reasons; a proposed event does not acquire an approval token by being written to B5.

**Event versus prose revision:** Changing the style of a sentence without changing accepted fictional events is an A text revision; changing who was injured, who heard the message, where an artifact was left or who returned from the field is an event delta. Both can invalidate downstream derivatives. A character may hold a mistaken belief; record narrator-level accepted facts separately from character knowledge.

### 6.2 Deterministic projection algorithm

1. Read the actual episode accepted head, parent episode/chapter carry-forward and effective character/technology canon with proven IDs/editions; capture `base_revision`.
2. Read accepted nonrevoked events and their authenticated decisions in **accepted revision/sequence order**. Preserve fictional effective timestamps as attributes; never reorder a later correction before its antecedent merely because its story-time is a flashback.
3. Exclude `PROPOSED`, `REJECTED`, not-yet-effective, withdrawn or unresolved invalid events. The word “latest” is not acceptance evidence.
4. Validate every accepted event's causal predecessors, person/mission physical positions, required knowledge, artifacts, permission and effective technology. An absent ancestor or contradictory accepted delta becomes a logged `BLOCKING` conflict rather than a guessed reconciliation.
5. Apply effective deltas in the authorized sequence to separate character, mission, arc, day and episode projections. Process approved supersession, correction and compensating events without destructive mutation; recompute all dependent later effects as needed.
6. Record projection proof: accepted head ID, contributing event IDs, excluded proposals/corrections, source and canon editions, computation/capture time and unresolved exception refs. Carry forward only this verified accepted state to the next daily draft.
7. Keep today's newly written events as a **proposed overlay** with their conditional post-state and exact A source revision; they are not committed accepted events until an authorized editorial decision and verified persistence actually occur.
8. Recheck that an authorized rights withdrawal or lawful erasure causes appropriate redaction, tombstone, permission restriction, derivative staleness and publication review; preserve limited audit information only as lawful.

**Invariant tests:** a stranded person stays stranded until an **accepted** rescue/return event; a person cannot know a private field transmission before receipt; no character can simultaneously have incompatible accepted physical locations; injury recovery and project completion require actual accepted evidence appropriate to fiction or independent real operations; a second Friday does not auto-retrieve field crew; an episode/chapter change does not auto-promote drafts to series canon.

### 6.3 Approval transition and correction semantics

`PROPOSED → ACCEPTED` requires an actual authorized editorial decision on the exact version/event and, where persistence is claimed, a verified write; `PROPOSED → REJECTED` retains a reason; accepted material later corrected uses a **new** approved event with explicit `supersedes`, `correction_of` or compensating semantics; a historical event's earlier accepted status remains verifiable subject to lawful retention. A model-generated decision table is a **recommendation**, not an authenticated human approval. Accepted fictional events are canon **within the scope approved**, not factual evidence of Koware's external activities or proof of formal global canon promotion.

## 7. Challenge, rights and derivative dependency graph

### 7.1 Challenge A, optional tier B, restricted C

Public Challenge A is a single, fair, story-anchored question and optional complete choices **literally at end** of Output A. Store exactly that public text/version, its A source and public release status in `challenge_public_register`. The term **internal B1–B8** never means optional audience `CHALLENGE_TIER_B`; keep them separate in all tooling. Optional challenge tier B tasks, the “43 Clues” idea, Service Cards, Buzz Codes, QR, scores, badges, Palmiers, myCred and reward/eligibility systems are future features requiring an independently authorized ruleset, privacy checks and service integration. A fictional achievement creates no real points.

Restricted challenge C is a solution/adjudication body only in independent secure custody. Not generated by ordinary A/B. B7/B8 can state safe nonrevealing key existence/absence and owner-controlled location **without** answer, hints, variants or computation. Follow-on daily challenge reveal/competition obligations must be an **approved editorial schedule**; day #10 does not auto-publish withheld keys or award prizes.

### 7.2 Exact upstream dependencies and staleness

Canonical chain: approved calendar, locked canon and source-use rights → accepted A revision → exact public challenge revision → **separately commissioned** comic design/pages and media script/shot plan → actual rendered assets/audio/captions → actual assembled export → human approval and publication. Store `source_revision`, `source_digest` if measured, `canon_revision`, `permission_revision`, `challenge_revision`, asset revisions and creator/provider evidence. A storyboard is not a pixel render; an image prompt is not a video; a 2×2 collage is not proof of four separately extracted pages; a file link is not public release.

On change to story scene, rights, public question, appearance/canon, speaker/voice or mission location, traverse dependent asset graph and set affected drawings, dialogues, panel/sheet mappings, captions, shots, sound, exports and metadata to `STALE_SOURCE` or `STALE_REVALIDATION_REQUIRED` as supported by actual implementation. Log reason, old/new parent revision, affected scope, license and reviewer; revalidate neighboring frames only if affected. Do not claim regenerated assets without actual generation or award credits that no connected provider confirmed.
