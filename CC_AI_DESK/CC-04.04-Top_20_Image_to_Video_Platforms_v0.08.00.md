# CLASSIES CHRONICLES — CC-04.04
## Twenty Image-to-Video Platform Candidates and Renewable-Free-Access Verification Register
**Edition:** v0.08.00 candidate · **Research cutoff:** 2026-11-09 · **Classification:** internal service-planning reference, NOT a guarantee of current account eligibility.

## Purpose and an important qualification
The commission asks for *twenty platforms offering free **refreshing** image-to-video credits*. Research does **not** substantiate that exact promise for twenty providers at the same confidence level. This report supplies **twenty researched candidates**, sharply distinguishing demonstrated recurring access from conditional, geographically restricted, incompatible or unverified claims. It would be misleading to call every entry a verified daily-free image-to-video service. No account was logged in, no balance was read, no real reference uploaded and no video generation was completed during this document review.

**Eligibility definitions:** `A` = official evidence of a renewing/free recurring allowance with a photo-to-video or closely related visual-to-motion route; `B` = official evidence of recurring access but image-to-video eligibility, account route, model, geography or usage rights must be reconfirmed; `C` = mixed, promotional, third-party or insufficient renewal evidence; `D` = no demonstrated renewing free *video* entitlement, retained strictly as an alternative/negative control. `Daily`, `monthly`, `free access` and `trial` are **different**; tokens may be shared with image or other AI tasks. A credit count is not necessarily a clip count. Region, login, length, export watermark, refresh timezone and commercial rights may change without notice.

### Additional exclusions that should **not** be misrepresented as recurring free video
- **Runway** (https://runwayml.com): its public Free plan documents **125 one-time credits**, not a guaranteed daily or monthly replenishing video credit stream; valuable for trial only. Official: https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/50404627334547-Free-plan-details.
- **Pika** (https://pika.art): old search results mention free monthly credit counts, but its currently visible official pricing has reported **zero included free monthly credits**. Do not include based on outdated articles; recheck the official live pricing before listing as renewing.
- **ImagineArt** (https://www.imagine.art): daily free image credits do not imply that premium video models are free; current video model restrictions can require payment.
- **Microsoft Bing Video Creator** (https://www.bing.com): offers free standard-speed text-to-video in some surfaces, but image-to-video upload and image identity-preservation should **not** be assumed.

## Fit by Classies Chronicles shot type
**General cinematic storyboard motion:** verify Flow, Dreamina and PixVerse first. **Talking/gesturing character:** prioritize Viggle, HeyGen and Vidnoz when the current mode and rights permit it. **Illustrated comic close-ups:** test Firefly, Leonardo, SeaArt, Vidu and Canva if free image-to-video eligibility is confirmed. **Google-first workflow:** verify Google Flow; Google Photos and Vids are differentiated experiences with different capabilities/credit pools. **Budget contingency:** the remaining providers are exploratory and must not be counted as funded clips until actual allowance and reference support have been observed.

## Verification card — mandatory before each real clip
Record provider, actual official URL, access date UTC, account region, relevant plan, model/version, source of eligibility, specific still-image input support, character reference support, aspect/output resolution, fps, maximum seconds, generation credits, **free balance actually displayed**, reset type/time and observed next reset, processing time/queue, watermark, output download capability, retention/deletion, privacy/training opt-out, commercial/derivative rights, conflicting license rules, and any payment requirement. Record whether an actual test **succeeded**. If terms are unknown, record `NOT_VERIFIED` and do not claim the clip is funded or production-ready.

## Credits are not interchangeable
Let available per-provider free credits be `B`, estimated credits per success be `C`, and planned retry/QA reserve be `R`. Maximum safely budgeted attempts are `floor((B - R)/C)` **only after** actual B/C are known; if B or C is unknown, **no numerical assurance can be made**. A refresh promise is a quota policy, not an obligation to exhaust free credits or avoid licensing requirements. Never rotate accounts or automate quota evasion; do not upload internal challenge answers or unauthorized likenesses to providers.

## Publication status and maintenance
This provider register is a **research and routing candidate list**, not a claim that twenty recurring free image-to-video accounts were established. At each actual episode production, update the table based on official plan screens, current help documentation and real account observations, with change dates and a reviewer. Preserve historical snapshots rather than silently rewriting earlier entitlements. Product and price claims can change frequently.
