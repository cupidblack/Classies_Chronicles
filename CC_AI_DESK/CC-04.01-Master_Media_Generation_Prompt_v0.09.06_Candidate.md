For each real provider only when the values are actually known: `safe\_attempts = max(0, floor((visible\_balance - reserved\_retry\_credits) / credits\_per\_attempt))`. Preserve integer rounding and account-specific reset limits; if any required number is missing, **budget state `UNKNOWN`, no funded generation claim**. Plan attempts by narrative importance and identity risk, not by assumed equivalent credits. Prefer `EDITOR\_HOLD`/`EDITOR\_2D\_PAN\_ZOOM` for truly stationary shots. When all allowed providers fail/expire/out-of-credit, provide full source-specific prompts and editor timeline, or substitute an explicitly authorized still/stock plate; label each missing video `NOT\_GENERATED`.

Do not circumvent quotas by account farming, automated sign-up, watermarks removal or data-policy evasion. Free **usage** is never a blanket authorization for paid distribution, commercial monetization, unlicensed identity or unlimited sharing of an uploaded character reference.

## 28\. Audio track architecture and normalization

Preserve separable layers where possible: narration/dialogue, approved music, ambience, foley, SFX, optional audio description, mix master and program reference. ## 29\. Captions, transcript and readability

Create accurate final SRT/VTT only from the **locked final audio/edit**; inspect synchronization, language, names/pronunciation, speaker attribution and safe margins at playback size. If audio description is commissioned, time it around relevant speech without adding an answer hint or forbidden private fact.

## 30\. Export and measurement

Record actual file existence, path, format, frame size, frame rate, codec, duration, audio sample rate/channels, size, content hash, frame-black checks and final-frame/audio-tail review **only after inspecting the real export**.
