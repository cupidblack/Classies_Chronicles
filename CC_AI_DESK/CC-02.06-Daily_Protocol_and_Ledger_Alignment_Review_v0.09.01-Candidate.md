# 3. Cross-platform and historical adjudication

The earlier cross-platform compilation contained substantive and empty platform sections. Substantive architecture is adopted or qualified; empty sections carry no recommendation. Correlated repeats in generated platforms are not treated as independent validation.

Adopted principles:

- separate governance, execution, continuity and review responsibilities;
- perform one cumulative story merge rather than unrelated inserts;
- use publication-date verse and actual provider attribution;
- distinguish summary/detail Health & Safety codes;
- separate historical, current and probe evidence;
- preserve accepted canon and actual rights rather than inferring consent;
- keep real and fictional work distinct;
- preserve append-only events and append/supersede semantics;
- keep A, B and C technically isolated;
- require write/readback and no auto-publication.

Rejected or corrected principles:

- six public daily chronicles as the ordinary V1–V6 output;
- blind week-number-to-250-entry mapping as ratified law;
- future fictional headlines presented as real news;
- every source pasted as a public exposition block;
- fictional completion of real organizational work;
- automatic answer-key access or competitive publication;
- automatic comic/media generation from a daily prompt;
- using role slot 1–10 as the chapter 1–40 workday chart;
- treating a fresh probe response as a historical replay;
- assuming a GitHub file is a committed approved state.

# 8. Final recommendation

**Retain** CC-02.01, CC-02.05 and CC-02.06.

The recommended architecture is:

- master = policy and authority;
- daily protocol = one-run execution;
- context merge = cumulative story transformation;
- ledger/handover = episode-owned state and records;
- alignment review = compatibility and adoption trace;
- specialist comic/media documents = separate commissioned derivatives.

The v0.09.01 package is a candidate refactor, not a ratification. The next safe sequence is: owner edition decision, calendar and ID freeze, study/role/rights resolution, controlled pilot, actual Q testing, secure ledger adoption and separately authorized archive/publication write.
