**Interpretation:** This is a *ranked research and routing directory*, not a false statement that twenty distinct providers are verified to deliver daily renewing, unlimited, 16:9 image-to-video clips free and commercially licensed in Ghana. Evidence tiers: **A** = first-party recurring free visual-to-video generation or free request allowance documented, though current login/rights still need validation; **B** = some first-party recurring free quota and video or image animation route, with eligibility/model/cost/region conditions; **C** = marketed or historically free video but no reliable first-party standing renewal guarantee for target account; **D** = no established recurring free video route, shown only as a negative control (not counted as suitable funded production). This edition replaces the weaker unverified v0.08.00 routing assumption and adds **Magic Hour** as a documented daily-free image-to-video candidate.

## Shot-based shortlisting
For a standard CC cinematic film, test only **current account-eligible** Google Flow, Dreamina and PixVerse first; add Magic Hour and Viggle for controlled five-second segments, Leonardo/Vidu/SeaArt for stylized motion. For a credible talking character requiring a legally permitted likeness and voice, investigate HeyGen/Vidnoz but avoid treating avatar puppetry as an accepted live-action actor. Stationary shots are often better and safer animated deterministically in CapCut rather than spent against AI credits. Only use a provider after the real model's output ratio, free quota, upload restrictions, watermark and commercial rights pass the target job.

## Mandatory per-provider verification card
For each actual attempt preserve: `checked_at_utc`; product landing and exact generator URL; logged-in country/region and verified plan; model and selected mode; source of free grant; grant interval, reset rule and observed balance; per-attempt **displayed** cost; actual reference image slot count; character/scene appearance controls; duration/aspect/native output/fps; queue and export; visible watermark; allowed derivative, public and monetized distribution terms; provider privacy/training/retention; payment/phone requirement; observed result, output path/hash; and actual UTC next reset **if displayed**. Record `NOT_VERIFIED` rather than numerical confidence when inaccessible. Do not farm accounts or remove required branding.

## Time sensitivity and decision rule
Product terms may change *while this document is in use*. The observed documentation was checked on 19 November 2026 UTC, not a permanent representation of Ghana eligibility. Rank is based on plausibility, narrative fit and renewal evidence; it is not a performance benchmark. For a real commission, **actual rights + actual I2V support + actual 16:9 model + observed balance + total credit estimate** outrank this static ranking. If any of those are absent, choose a lawful non-generative fallback or leave clips as `NOT_GENERATED`.
