## 4. Error/risk and manual verification register
- **Dynamic credit claims:** provider availability, free renewal, region, commercial license, model pricing and daily allowance fluctuate. CC-04.04 explicitly distinguishes verified recurring pathways from conditional and unverified candidates. **Twenty verified recurring I2V providers are not established**.
- **Google-first option:** Google Flow official help has described 50 free credits per day, but availability, cost per model/clip and peak restrictions may change. Record the actual account and current help terms before budgeting.
- **Collage resolution:** a 4×4 collage at only 2048×2048 cannot yield 16 native 1080×1920 pages; reject rather than silently upscale labels. The tool can slice equal unguttered grids and annotate provenance, not visually segment arbitrary comic layouts.
- **Canvas conversion:** `fit` makes an undistorted portrait-on-widescreen stage with blurred side background; `cover` can lose content. Neither grants permissions, removes speech bubbles, or invents adjacent scenery.
- **Identity:** use a real approved character asset (and real-person consent where applicable). This package provides a lookup protocol rather than fictional character photos.
- **Runtime specific prompts:** examples are complete generic motion treatments; actual clip prompts cannot honestly incorporate unsupplied episode action, unverified actor identity, image URL or model settings.
- **Cross-suite internal numbering:** CC-02 ledger/review physical filename numbers shifted while logical IDs inside some documents did not; retain both IDs and resolve under owner-controlled revision.
- **QA versus acceptance:** passing 30 static/synthetic tests is **not** a pass on the 30 media QA gates, 30 pilot scenarios, rights review, frame fidelity or final video delivery.
