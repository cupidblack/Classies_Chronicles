## 1. Method and evidentiary distinctions

The proposal files supplied the creative baseline; the master/daily/Context Merge/Ledger/Comic/Media source suite supplied the operational constraints; the two platform compilations supplied comparison, cautions and alternative wording. For every claim, distinguish **an actually inspected public resource**, a **self-report inside a prior model response**, an **operating proposal**, and an **owner-ratified decision**. An external platform's earlier assertion that it could not access another file is a historical observation about that platform, **not proof the URL is currently broken**. The later compilation 5 was directly readable in this review and includes substantive ChatGPT, GitHub and Claude sections; other marked responses were `NULL`. Compilation 4 was also readable; do not adopt a prior platform's inability to load its complete contents as a universal access finding. Both are advisory sources, not authoritative production policy.

Public GitHub branch paths were inspected as retrieved source snapshots, not pinned immutable commit IDs. As a result, this review does not claim a repository-approved implementation state or that all historical linked assets in those long compilations were individually fetched. In particular, the 5 inspected `Identity_Logos` files do **not** prove exhaustive review of every asset in the directory, and image viewing does **not** establish copyright or trademark clearance.

## 4. Recommendations from the platforms — accepted, qualified or rejected

The two compilations contain varied platform outputs. A practical convergence gives them shared but not authoritative standing:

- **Accepted/adapted:** a single master router with specialist ownership; explicit `REVIEW_ONLY`; unbroken V1–V6 semantic synthesis; first-Monday chapter workday arithmetic; event-sourced episode continuity; four 2×2 9:16 comic sheets when commissioned; separate 16:9 media and accurate timing; exact Q/C/M quality codes; permission-scoped identities and source truth; robust no-auto-write; retrospective/future-research separation.
- **Accepted with qualification:** advanced Edubeam physics and cross-time comms (fictional and era-gated); seven-year study recurrence (framework preserved but explicit week mapping remains pending); serial role rotation (real chart controls); New Legon HQ five-building setting (possible fictional canon, not established reality); first-collage image style (not inherited wrong geometry); proposed Classies wordmark (not approved branding).
- **Deferred or rejected as unconditional obligations:** generating all ten days for a single prompt; automatically writing files to GitHub; auto-promoting proposed canon; treating a source proposal as staff consent; forcing twenty headlines into story prose; requiring every episode to end in a successful team return; launching real reward credit systems; assuming every listed video service gives daily credits; creating the challenge answer key in an unrestricted document; interpreting a nominal 20-minute movie as equal to unedited 10×160s clips.
- **Not asserted as validated:** current provider free-credit balances, exact project calendar configuration, ratified voice rights, private diaries, acceptance of visual material, existence of an answer vault, readback of a GitHub commit or actual quality of generated comic/video assets. These are runtime/deployment matters.

## 7. Limits of review and decisions for ratification

A source-level review can verify the content of files opened here and reconcile conflicting **textual instructions**; it cannot establish permissions on behalf of identifiable people, inspect a private calendar not provided, test actual paid rendering accounts or perform an archive write. Public URL contents can change after retrieval. This package's brand advice is a recommendation from inspected images, not a completed licensed visual-identity system. The correct final authority remains the actual owner/editor, current production calendar, current source rights and accepted canon.

The main master §52 lists **twelve ratification decisions**. High-priority dependencies are: calendar/timezone; source-ID crosswalk; explicit Bible week-to-entry date map; actual character and source rights; current mission/Edubeam canon; secret answer custody; 16-page comic default/branding; truthful media runtime/provider constraints; and actual repository storage/release policy.
