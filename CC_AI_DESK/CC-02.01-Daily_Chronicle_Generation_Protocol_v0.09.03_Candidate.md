## 4. Phase 2 — resolve local chapter and episode calendar exactly

The adopted calendar and production time zone control. The **Millennium Probe phase is not** an adopted publishing calendar.

1. Resolve approved chapter phase start `phase_start_local` and end boundary in the **production IANA time zone**, preserving offsets and whether the end is inclusive. Distinguish date-only from timestamp boundaries.
2. Compute `anchor` as the **first Monday on/after** phase-start local civil date. A phase that begins midweek does not begin Chronicle #1 on that Wednesday.
3. Calculate the integer local-civil-date difference `offset_days = target_local_date − anchor_date`; reject negative offsets and target dates outside the effective chapter.
4. Calculate `block = offset_days // 14` and `block_day = offset_days % 14`; only `block` 0–3 is an ordinary four-episode chapter candidate.
5. Require the **entire two-week calendar allocation** for that episode to fit approved chapter bounds, including the second Friday (`anchor + 14*block + 11 days`) and any owner-required full-block condition. Do not pretend that an incomplete fourth episode has ten eligible days. Record phase-end ambiguity for editorial resolution.
6. Accept `block_day` only in `{0,1,2,3,4,7,8,9,10,11}`. For 0–4: `chronicle_slot = block_day+1`; for 7–11: `chronicle_slot = block_day-1`; other offsets are weekends and have no numbered ordinary chronicle.
7. Set `episode_ordinal = block+1` and `chapter_workday = 10*block + chronicle_slot` (range 1–40). **Role checks use chapter_workday**, not chronicle slot, Gregorian month day or Probe `phase_index`.
8. Cross-check accepted archive identifiers and aliases, existing day records, weekday, role-chart `Day/Days` text and chronology. Preserve historical `S00_T05`, `S00_T05_E01`, `S00_CH05_E02`, `S00CH01E01DAY01` patterns as observed, not assumed interchangeable.

**Deterministic offset examples, not live calendar assertions:** offsets 0,4,7,11,14,18,21,25,28,39,42,53 map respectively to chapter days 1,5,6,10,11,15,16,20,21,30,31,40; offsets 5,6,12,13 are weekends. A phase starting Wednesday and an episode ending past an approved phase boundary must fail assignment unless an explicitly documented exception authorizes another arrangement.

**Gate.** Q02/Q04/Q06 capture the real calendar authority and ID/role evidence; a missing approved phase or unresolved collision that changes installment identity means `BLOCKED` for a definitive numbered daily part. Owner-authorized provisional work can remain an unnumbered, clearly nonpublishable experimental draft; it is not an accepted archive installment.
