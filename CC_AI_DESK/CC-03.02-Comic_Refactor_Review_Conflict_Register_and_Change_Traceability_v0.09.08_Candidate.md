## 2. Summary determination

The user's proposals are coherent and valuable but need four architectural clarifications to be enforceable:

**(A) A prepared palette does not coerce an image model's native output geometry.** Prebuilding a 4320×7680 sheet and four 2160×3840 page canvases locks the **assembly**, not the neural renderer. If a provider returns a 1024×1536 raster, it still returns 2:3. Therefore an exact-page-native workflow plus a mechanical measured acceptance gate is necessary; provider failure must be flagged rather than hidden by stretching/upscaling.

**(B) Four 9:16 pages in a 2×2 grid yield a 9:16 sheet.** `page 2160×3840 → sheet 4320×7680`, whereas `page 1080×1920 → sheet 2160×3840`. The proposed large sheet contains higher-resolution pages than the minimum. It is not a substitute for source-detail inspection.

**(C) An episode's first chronicle may also be its episode opener.** Both cannot claim a separate overlapping cover-only header; a **single 25% P01 header** satisfies both the episode's 25–33⅓% and daily chronicle's 20–25% contracts. It has one selected Classies logo and one episode title, continues actual story action and carries the longer daily folio.

**(D) Season/chapter full covers and episode/day integrated opening pages are different asset classes.** Covers are reusable standalone `COVER_STEP` artifacts outside the four-page narrative sheets. The first-page header should not consume P01 as a cover-only page; no repeated logos or headers on later pages.

This amendment also formally proposes **N8** as the comic default, **N4** for genuinely short daily sources, and **N=4k** for explicit commissions or warranted expanded treatment. The previously selected N16 for earlier editions remains locked. The broader CC-01 master currently cited by v0.09.05 records a conflicting N16 default; owner adoption and alignment remain a separate governance action.

### 5.1 Geometry proof and negative examples

Let a page be `(W,H)` with 16W = 9H. Two pages side-by-side and two rows yield `(2W,2H)`. Then `16(2W)=9(2H)`; ratio is still 9:16. Fixed page sizes must be integer pixel dimensions and should be multiples of 9 and 16 respectively; **2160/3840** is a valid premium instance.

| Test sample | Page geometry | 2×2 geometry | Expected |
|---|---|---|---|
| Four native/final 2160×3840 pages | 2160×3840 | 4320×7680 | Geometry PASS |
| Four native/final 1080×1920 pages | 1080×1920 | 2160×3840 | Geometry PASS at minimum |
| Four 540×960 pages | 540×960 | 1080×1920 | **FAIL minimum page size**; ratio alone PASS |
| One 1024×1536 sheet split into four | 512×768 | 1024×1536 | **FAIL ratio and size** |
| Four 2:3 images warped to 2160×3840 | Export may show 2160×3840 | 4320×7680 | Pixel ratio PASS but **content/provenance QA FAIL** |
| One 4320×7680 canvas with only one image covering all cells | No four distinct full pages | 4320×7680 | **FAIL page identity/count** |
| Season cover inserted as top-left cell | One cell is cover-only | Any | **FAIL four-story-pages** |
