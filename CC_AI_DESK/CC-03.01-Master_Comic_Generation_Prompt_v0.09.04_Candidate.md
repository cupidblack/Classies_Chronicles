## 8. Binding artistic grammar: realistic core / expressive accent
**A. Realistic core, always.** Retain believable human facial structure, eye spacing, skin color/undertone, facial hair, braids, natural hair, fingers, limbs, body scale, clothing fit, perspective, material finish, architecture and genuine light direction. Every main character should be immediately recognizable across panels, days and different image tools. **B. Illustrated finish, always.** Use clean but not sterile contour hierarchy, tactile rich color, controlled sharpened focal faces, nuanced color grading, professional shadow modeling, camera depth, grounded Ghanaian streets and architecture, appropriate Malay/Kuala Lumpur scenes, luminous realistic laboratory displays, balanced sharp details and readable visual story cues. Use selective fine hatch, edge glow, ink flicks, painterly textures, comic speed/impact emphasis as an art finish—not uniform posterization, plastic 3D or synthetic toy sheen.

**C. Toony expression, only when earned.** With a comic *reaction beat*, momentarily allow wide popping eyes, a playful jaw drop, eyebrows springing up, a hand or fingertip swelling **symbolically** after a comic knock, sweat drops, a comic zigzag, a bulging comedic forehead, exaggerated squint, motion streak or intentionally contorted expression. Restore the original locked appearance in the next ordinary panel. **Do not use humorous distortion for grief, actual severe injury, an emergency evacuation, private trauma, violence, disability, sacred ritual or a factual safety warning unless the story explicitly and responsibly warrants it.**

**D. Emotion and character.** Distinguish curiosity, skepticism, discernment, fatigue, ambition, kindness, tension, controlled surprise and humor. Give hands meaningful, correctly shaped interactions with actual props. Use selective motion/comic marks to show action, not to decorate a static shot with generic lightning.

**E. Exclusions.** No default anime, chibi, chubby toys, emoji heads, flat vector, disembodied collage heads, off-model celebrity lookalikes, unreadable wall-of-text, inconsistent facial age/skin, oversaturated generic sci-fi UI, fake/unsafe official signage, repeated stock establishing shots, borrowed superhero or manga franchise visual signatures, or unsolicited photorealistic face swap. These exclusions **do not** prohibit a brief source-justified comic exaggeration under C.

Each complete page must advance a **distinct story beat, consequence, decision, revelation, action, reflection or justified transition**, not a duplicate viewpoint of identical wording and action. One call generates only the planned N once; further variations require a subsequent express request or recorded localized failure repair.

## 12. Geometry, page pixels and 2×2 feasibility
For a true individual page `16 × pixel_width = 9 × pixel_height`. Use portrait 1080×1920 original pixels or a higher genuinely generated 9:16 equivalent; below-target dimensions are **lower-resolution drafts** unless separately approved for the actual delivery. A model's **outer** 1024×1536 image is 2:3, not 9:16. A 2×2 1080×1920 outer sheet has four 540×960 cells: each can be 9:16 but **none is native 1080×1920**. Upscaling does not restore lost lettering detail and cannot be reported as native resolution.

With **zero exterior margins and zero inter-cell gutters**, a 2×2 sheet of equal W×H pages has size 2W×2H and the **same aspect ratio** as its cells. Thus 4 × 1080×1920 -> 2160×3840 **and the sheet is itself exactly 9:16 portrait**. Coordinates for sheet width 2160, height 3840: TL `(0,0,1080,1920)`, TR `(1080,0,2160,1920)`, BL `(0,1920,1080,3840)`, BR `(1080,1920,2160,3840)`; cropping boxes use left/top inclusive and right/bottom exclusive pixel endpoints. The 2×2 sheet is NOT 9:8; 9:8 would result from a **2×1** row of two 9:16 pages.

If practical gutters or margins are necessary, define a *measured* sheet geometry and check **each crop** individually: `sheetW = 2W + inner_vertical_gutter + 2horizontal_margin`; `sheetH = 2H + inner_horizontal_gutter + 2vertical_margin`. Additional sheet space usually changes outer aspect unless the margins/gutters are proportioned; prefer **zero-gutter export sheets with visible gutters inside each page design**. Never crop through page content, silently resize cell width differently from its height, or add a decorative border that turns 9:16 into another ratio.

**Renderer fallback contract:** If direct four-page generation cannot reliably deliver 2160×3840 or its genuinely legible equivalent, generate **four separate original 9:16 pages at real supported size** and mechanically combine into one measured 2×2 sheet. If the renderer can only output a lower resolution, disclose the limitation and seek an authorized different delivery specification; do not misrepresent quality. A single 4×4 sheet for N=16 is **not** the default and does not meet the specified four-sheet delivery merely by being cropped later.

## 14. Individual page composition and comic page identity

Read English left-to-right within a row, then top-to-bottom; across a 2×2 sheet the complete *pages* read TL→TR→BL→BR. Keep source-backed captions high enough to be visible under mobile story overlays. Do not put numbered scene IDs, draft labels, internal QA marks or output status in public art. Preserve verified accents, languages, numerals, dates, Scripture refs, signs and terminology. Use exact source dialogue when quoting; new bridging dialogue must not alter plot and needs editorial permission. Never fabricate text in real safety documents or historical evidence.

**Text-first safety:** For exact/long messages, dates, quotes and Challenge A use controlled vector/type overlay after art rendering when possible. ## 21. Controlled expression effects and the return-to-model check
Tag exceptional expressions per **panel** rather than per batch. Example beat classes: surprise → eye widening and small comic spark; confusion → temporary elastic eyebrows or subtle head squiggle; comedic collision → symbolic enlarged fingers/motion star while preserving plausible arm/hand continuity; sudden discovery → quick zoom or speed accents; relief → light smile and softened contour. The next realistic frame must recover the actor's established features. Avoid deforming speech text, factual evidence, printed emergency notices or source-significant objects. Log the allowed exaggeration so a future editor can distinguish intentional style from a misshapen hand/face rendering failure.

## 22. Lettering, local corrections and defect containment
Inspect the actual generated cells at **full export**, not only the composite thumbnail. Identify incorrect text, doubled/missing fingers, wrong speaker tail, mislabeled project, stale character appearance, exact source disagreement, fictional safety directions, unsupported technology and accidental restricted information. For an art-only error, repair only the offending page image or panel, not an already correct neighboring sheet. For a source/challenge/rights/canon correction, mark actual dependents stale and revalidate only changed pages plus cross-boundary state. Maintain old revised asset checksums in the internal audit; never hide a second rendition as the original first pass.

Typography repair must be verified in the resulting *rendered* JPEG/PNG crop. The previous prompt saying “large font” is not evidence that the image lettering is large or correct. Do not rasterize new lettering over text already hallucinated in the art unless the underlying artifact is removed/covered cleanly.

## 23. Assembly, slicing and aspect audits
Use verified full-resolution individual pages with identical W,H and suitable color mode. Verify unique page IDs, expected 9:16 geometry, correct actor/beat order and absence of conflicts. Assemble pages zero-gutter into 2W×2H; do not resample or stretch. Compute original file SHA-256 hashes and assembled sheet hash when true bytes exist. When slicing a **known correctly measured sheet**, crop exactly the four page bounding boxes defined in §12; verify crop sizes and no lettering clipped at boundaries. The optional companion tool `cc_comic_sheet_tool_v0.09.04.py` implements geometry/manifest bookkeeping and a cross-sheet `audit` of all actual P01–PN page files but **cannot judge aesthetics, rights, factual accuracy or source fidelity**.

Always test sheet-level and individual-page **distinctness**: identifiers, mapped beats and obvious duplicated imagery, with perceptual similarity only as a review flag (lighting/series-style similarities are not automatically duplicates). The image file containing four cells is one *sheet artifact*; its four crops are four *page artifacts* **only after actually exported**. Report separate counts and hashes.

## 24. Quality tiers, truth about technical capacity and QA outcome
Record the **actual** renderer/provider, native produced dimensions, original format, tool transformations, cropping, post-lettering, repair history and intended viewing service. A reference screenshot, low-resolution cell, upscaled preview or transcribed storyboard is **never** a native, legible exported page. A source asset may pass artistic inspection yet fail text/sizing/privacy or 9:16. A real rendered sheet can have `RENDERED_DRAFT` while `C11`, `C13`, `C19` remain `NOT_CHECKED` until measured pixel QA. Apply all exact C01–C22 identifiers from §27, with evidence, failure disposition and reviewer.

If direct four-page sheets fail quality, switch only to the authorized four-native-pages-then-assemble path. If that is not technically feasible, report a partial outcome rather than call a weak 1024×1536 sheet “publish-ready 2160×3840.”

## 25. Nine ordered production stages

5. **Storyboard and style lock:** fully populate §17 for every page; prepare style/character cards and deterministic text plan. Stop here for `COMIC_STORYBOARD`.

## 28. Failure and recovery policy
| Observed condition | Response |
|---|---|
| Unreadable/uncertain source A or conflicting accepted state | Hold asserted full adaptation; perform permitted bounded planning or report blocked scenes. |

| Sample image 2:3 or direct 2×2 too small | Recompose true 9:16 full pages and then assemble; never claim fake native quality. |
| Duplicate page, scene/panel order, missing page ID | Reject counted inventory and resolve only affected page/batch identities; do not output alternatives. |
| Hallucinated letters, labels or missing challenge words | Correct deterministic type on actual exported file, recheck mobile-size pixels; challenge conflict blocks final page. |
| Restricted key hinted or revealed | Withdraw/block public assets and cleanse affected prompt/public metadata; use independent authorized incident handling. |
| Story/canon/permission revised | Mark exact dependent page/sheet/export stale and revalidate/revise only impacted components. |
| Viewer has no binary pixels or renderer inaccessible | Report visual/pixel QA `NOT_CHECKED` and never invent a finished output or release-ready status. |
| A direct 2×2 sheet contains four posters, not four pages | Reject sheet and rebuild from four independent fully storyboarded pages. |
