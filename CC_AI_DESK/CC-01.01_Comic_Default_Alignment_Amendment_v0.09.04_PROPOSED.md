# CLASSIES CHRONICLES — Proposed CC-UPMP Comic Alignment Amendment
## Candidate patch to CC-01.01 / CC-UPMP v0.09.01 · prepared 19 November 2026 UTC

**Status: PROPOSED CHANGE ONLY.** This file does not modify the existing `CC-01.01-Unified_Production_Master_Prompt_v0.09.01_Candidate.md` on GitHub, does not authorize publication and has no effective date. Its purpose is to remove a precise default-count disagreement before the new CC-03.01 v0.09.04 comic specialist is accepted as a recurring production contract.

### 1. Amend §42 opening paragraph and count-specific guidance

Replace the paragraph establishing sixteen pages as the ordinary default with this **exact proposed governing rule**:

> **Eight-page daily mobile comic default.** For an ordinary separately commissioned Classies Chronicles daily comic without an explicit page count, the **new adopted default shall be N=8 distinct complete 9:16 portrait pages** in exactly **two** sequential zero-gutter 2×2 sheets (P01–P04, P05–P08). The governing selected specialist is `CC-03.01 / CC-MCGP v0.09.04` once adopted in a recorded decision. A sufficiently expansive commission may explicitly specify **N=16** for four sheets or **N=20** for five sheets; any exact authorized positive N overrides the ordinary default. No page is repeated to reach an arbitrary count. The final complete approved public Challenge A appears exactly once at the literal bottom of page PN and nothing visible follows or sits below it. For any N divisible by four, create exactly N/4 complete 2×2 portrait sheets in row-major TL/TR/BL/BR order. If N is not divisible by four and sheets-only delivery is mandatory, resolve the format/count conflict without inventing filler pages. Every actual complete page remains 9:16, and a zero-gutter 2×2 sheet of such equal pages is itself 9:16. Native individual 1080×1920 pages compose a 2160×3840 2×2 sheet; never mislabel low-resolution sheet crops or upscales as such native originals. Preserve source revisions, accepted continuity, permissions, truthful status and C01–C22 evidence.

Keep §42's other detailed requirements for authentic image inspection, art quality, cross-sheet character consistency, deterministic lettering, safe margins and honest export status, replacing only text that still implies 16 is the unqualified ordinary default.

### 2. Amend §46 C18 inventory sentence

Retain exact identifier `C18_COUNT_INVENTORY` and replace its hard-coded N=16 description with:

> **`C18_COUNT_INVENTORY`:** For the exact authorized N, verify exactly N distinct real complete 9:16 portrait pages with stable IDs P01–PN, no duplicate beat/page variants, honest asset-revision history and, when N is divisible by four, exactly N/4 distinct full 2×2 portrait sheet files with row-major four-page mapping and measured crop/export proof. N=8 requires 2 sheets; N=16 requires 4 sheets; N=20 requires 5 sheets. A sheet file does not by itself prove four individual page files were exported.

No C ID is renamed; C01–C22 remain stable. Artistic style, toony expression, character look and readability remain subchecks of existing C06/C10/C12/C13/C19.

### 3. Update other master mentions without touching unrelated rules

At §52 item 10 and any analogous unqualified references to “standard sixteen-page comic” or “four standard 2×2 sheets,” say **“default eight-page comic in two 2×2 sheets; extended sixteen-page in four sheets or explicitly commissioned N”**. Keep media duration arithmetic and unrelated sixteen-clip audiovisual rules unchanged: comic pages, video acts and shots are different units. Retain owner consent/rights/continuity constraints and the rule that an exact authorized page count overrides a default. Preserve the old master file/version intact as historic source; issue a new governed master revision and record its approval/effective date if adopted.

### 4. Compatibility and acceptance sequence

An authorized editor should (1) approve N=8 as ordinary default, (2) approve the targeted CC-01.01 master amendment as a new real version, (3) select `CC-03.01 v0.09.04` and its two companions, (4) select effective CC-02 editions, (5) run a real eight-page pilot, (6) record genuine pass/fail C-gate evidence, and only then authorize release/persistence as appropriate. A one-off commission for exactly eight pages works even before policy change under the existing master exact-N exception; that does not globally adopt N=8. No automated edit or adoption is asserted here.
