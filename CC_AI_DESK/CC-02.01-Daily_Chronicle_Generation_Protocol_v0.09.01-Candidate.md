> **EXECUTABLE RUNBOOK DIRECTIVE.** Use this document only for an expressly commissioned `DAILY_CHRONICLE` or a clearly labeled controlled run fixture. It implements the authority and quality rules of `CC-01.01` without repeating or overriding that master.

# 1. Necessity and authority

This runbook remains necessary because the master defines policy while this document defines the ordered operational sequence. It must not be used to decide that the candidate master is effective. In every run, resolve the actual scope-specific owner authority, effective master edition, chapter calendar, canon, rights and storage ACLs before story drafting.

The only implicit authority is source retrieval permitted for the commissioned mode. Retrieval is not permission to publish, depict, rewrite accepted canon or write to storage. If no live owner authority can be established, stop at `BLOCKED` or, for a review-only request, finish only documentary work.

# 2. Independent state axes

Maintain separate values for:

- **Process:** `REQUEST_CLASSIFIED`, `PREFLIGHT`, `GO`, `GO_WITH_OMISSIONS`, `BLOCKED`, `EVIDENCE_LOCKED_FOR_DRAFT`, `DRAFTED`, `AWAITING_EDITORIAL_REVIEW`.
- **Editorial:** `UNREVIEWED`, `CHANGES_REQUESTED`, `ACCEPTED`, `LOCKED`, `REJECTED`.
- **Persistence:** `UNSAVED`, `DRAFTED_NOT_COMMITTED`, `WRITE_AUTHORIZED`, `COMMIT_ATTEMPTED`, `COMMITTED_VERIFIED`, `WRITE_FAILED`, `CONFLICT_REQUIRES_RECONCILIATION`.
- **Release:** `NOT_ELIGIBLE`, `RELEASE_ELIGIBLE`, `PUBLICATION_AUTHORIZED`, `PUBLISHED_VERIFIED`.
- **Derivative:** `NOT_COMMISSIONED`, `PLANNED`, `STORYBOARDED`, `PROMPTS_CREATED`, `ASSETS_RENDERED_VERIFIED`, `ASSEMBLED_VERIFIED`, `QA_PASSED`, `APPROVED`, `STALE_SOURCE`.

# 3. Phase 0 — classify and pin authority

1. Determine the exact primary mode. Separate any expressly commissioned subordinate mode; do not infer it from this runbook.
2. Resolve the target production civil date or obtain it from the actual approved calendar; preserve the approved IANA timezone and any explicit historical target-date basis.
3. Resolve the working master, daily protocol, ledger, current continuity, character, role, calendar and rights authorities. A filename or a more recent number does not establish effectiveness without owner/authority evidence.
4. Record whether A is a `DRAFT`, `REVIEW_COPY`, `ACCEPTED_REVISION` or `RELEASE_ELIGIBLE` artifact. Generation alone creates no acceptance or release state.
5. Verify that public A can be physically separated from internal B and restricted C.

**Exit:** `REQUEST_CLASSIFIED` and `PREFLIGHT` are truthful. Otherwise set `BLOCKED`, name the decision owner and stop.

# 4. Phase 1 — acquire the source snapshot

For every meaningful source, preserve:

`source_id`, `source_type`, `canonical_url_or_id`, `repository_revision`, `publisher_or_custodian`, `retrieval_timestamp_utc`, `observed_as_of`, `target_story_date`, `claimed_fact`, `claim_id`, `rights_scope`, `evidence_status`, `conflict_refs` and `integrity_digest` when calculated.

Permitted evidence states are `AVAILABLE`, `STALE`, `FAILED`, `NOT_AUTHORIZED`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `CONTRADICTED` and `MISSING_WITH_REASON`. Missing evidence must not become a fabricated quote, article, passage, lesson, person, status or deadline.

## 4.1 Mandatory source domains

- Effective master/protocol/ledger/continuity and explicit owner decisions.
- Approved production calendar, timezone and chapter/episode slot authority.
- Existing accepted chronicle and accepted continuity event head.
- Actual Eternal Dreamfall weekly study plan and separately approved date-to-entry mapping.
- Publication-date Verse of the Day, with exact provider, reference and translation.
- Relevant Buzzjuice Health & Safety course/lesson, distinguishing summary and detailed course codes.
- Three spatial-temporal streams: historical, present and probe, with independent source windows and locations.
- Approved character canons, aliases, real-person portrayals and authorized diary/calendar sources.
- Organizational chart revision and relevant real Koware projects/action plans.
- Public events, weather and relevant mission/business context only from evidence that is usable for the story date.
- Any challenge prior obligation. Never retrieve or generate an answer key for an ordinary run.

## 4.2 Calendar and context snapshot

Retrieve an episode context only when the actual approved calendar exists. Use P/H/F independently:

- **P — production/present:** approved story/publication civil date, timezone and Koware present world.
- **H — historical/reflective:** endpoint `iso` and separately accepted Enka historical-reference location(s).
- **F — field/probe:** endpoint `probe_iso` and accepted target location; endpoint telemetry is not a chapter calendar.

Preserve retrieval UTC, source offset and converted civil date. If a field is absent or contradictory, record the exact failure instead of substituting a date, location or oscillation value.

# 5. Phase 2 — resolve slot and role identity

Follow CC-UPMP §§6–9 and the effective approved calendar.

1. Resolve the target local civil date and timezone.
2. Resolve approved phase start/end and inclusive/exclusive convention.
3. Set the anchor to the first Monday on or after chapter start unless an approved exception explicitly changes it.
4. Compute `elapsed_days = target_date - anchor` using whole local civil days.
5. Reject negative offsets, weekend ordinary slots, gaps and episode blocks that do not fit the approved chapter end.
6. `episode_index = floor(elapsed_days / 14) + 1`; `block_day = elapsed_days mod 14`.
7. Map `block_day` 0–4 to Chronicle 1–5 and 7–11 to Chronicle 6–10.
8. Map chapter working day `10 × (episode_index - 1) + chronicle_slot`, then use the effective role chart's 1–40 values—not the story slot or the day of month.

Illustrative arithmetic fixtures, not live episode claims:

| Elapsed days | Episode | Chronicle | Chapter workday |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| 4 | 1 | 5 | 5 |
| 7 | 1 | 6 | 6 |
| 11 | 1 | 10 | 10 |
| 14 | 2 | 1 | 11 |
| 28 | 3 | 1 | 21 |
| 42 | 4 | 1 | 31 |

A partial phase, unresolved chapter identity or missing calendar authority yields `CALENDAR_EXCEPTION` or `BLOCKED`; it does not yield a guessed public episode number.

# 6. Phase 3 — rebuild accepted pre-state

Read the last accepted chronicle and the accepted continuity projection. Build an immutable pre-state including:

- season, chapter, episode and stable/legacy IDs;
- all active characters and their accepted time/location/knowledge/relationships;
- active and deferred storylines;
- mission roster, Edubeam position, coordinates, charge, equipment, communications and retrieval obligations;
- real Koware project status and role-day data;
- prior public challenge status;
- approved unfulfilled obligations and the next safe scene dependencies.

Proposed events, draft scenes and this run's ideas may modify a projected state only as `PROPOSED`, never as accepted canon.

# 7. Phase 4 — run the cumulative merge

Perform one sequential narrative derivation. In ordinary daily mode, preserve a private transformation record; do not publish V1–V6 as six chronicles.

| Stage | Required source duty | Completion proof |
|---|---|---|
| V1 — Eternal Dreamfall Bible Study | Actual study allocation and a genuine, classified text or supported summary | Study dossier and a story seed preserving recognizable structure and motif |
| V2 — Eternal Dreamfall Chronicle | Publication-date verse and genuine provider/translation | A real moral/emotional lens changes a choice or consequence |
| V3 — Refreshed Eternal Dreamfall | Relevant actual Health & Safety course/lesson | A realistic hazard/control or recovery changes the plot |
| V4 — Headlined Eternal Dreamfall | Relevant historical, present and probe-era evidence | An actual typed source signal changes stakes, timing or setting |
| V5 — Classies Eternal Dreamfall | Accepted archive, canon and authorized diaries | Earlier events, knowledge or relationships alter today's action |
| V6 — Classies Chronicles | Verified Koware role/project reality and present human stakes | Real organizational pressure creates an earned episode beat |

Apply the eight-operation loop at every boundary: **Read → Decompose → Lock → Relate → Choose → Transform → Reconcile → Prove**. Hard anchors must survive unless an owner-authorized amendment exists. A source that has no causally relevant effect is omitted or logged as `REJECTED_FOR_STORY`, not forced into exposition.

# 8. Phase 5 — draft Output A

Produce exactly one coherent story for the one authorized day.

A must include:

1. the accepted or clearly provisional day/episode identification;
2. a concrete opening, desire, obstacle and consequential choice;
3. one coherent Causal A-plot and only useful relationships or B/C beats;
4. the real source functions in character actions rather than compliance exposition;
5. a readable resolution or earned next-episode continuation;
6. exactly one public Challenge A as the literal final audience-facing item.

Do not include internal source registers, B1–B8, answer keys, answer rationale, raw telemetry, repository revisions or a notice that B is hidden. No public text may convert fictional Koware work into real accomplishment or real-person claims into canon.

# 9. Phase 6 — prepare separate Output B1–B8

B is internal and access-controlled. The exact eight section order is:

- **B1 — Run and temporal manifest:** mode, date/timezone, governing editions, effective authority, ID crosswalk, P/H/F captures and locations, role index.
- **B2 — Research/source register:** source/claim IDs, study, verse, H&S, news/weather, permission and provenance states, V1–V6 source-to-beat trace.
- **B3 — Character, Koware role and project register:** canon/diary/likeness rights, aliased role, real-versus-fiction project status and owners.
- **B4 — Mission and technology state:** active roster, accepted locations/times, coordinates, Edubeam era capability, charge, equipment, communications and retrieval obligations.
- **B5 — Append-only continuity and story ledger:** accepted pre-state, scene-bound proposed events, conditional post-state, open arcs and correction/supersession links.
- **B6 — Portable production handover:** self-contained fifteen-part transfer. Include no challenge solution or restricted private data.
- **B7 — Derivative/media and challenge status:** only commissioned derivative work, exact source revision, public question status and stale-source flags.
- **B8 — Validation, exceptions and approval register:** exact Q01–Q16 results, evidence, blockers, decisions and true write/release state.

# 10. Phase 7 — run B8 QA Q01–Q16

Every gate requires a real observed result: `PASS`, `FAIL`, `NOT_RUN` or `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

| Gate | Core requirement |
|---|---|
| `Q01_SCOPE` | Exact authorized mode/date/output boundary |
| `Q02_CALENDAR` | Effective timezone, chapter/episode slots and 1–40 role index |
| `Q03_THREE_CLOCKS` | Independent P/H/F data and separate physical locations |
| `Q04_IDENTIFIERS` | Stable IDs, aliases and no invented archive path |
| `Q05_CANON` | Accepted character, era, relationship and mission state |
| `Q06_ROLE_PROJECT` | Actual chart revision and real/fiction/proposal separation |
| `Q07_STUDY` | Approved week anchor and actual or honestly omitted study text |
| `Q08_VERSE_HS` | Actual dated verse/provider and real H&S course/lesson |
| `Q09_RESEARCH` | Dated, sourced, geographically valid history/current/probe evidence |
| `Q10_RIGHTS` | Specific consent, privacy, diary, voice, likeness and distribution rights |
| `Q11_STORY` | Original, cumulative, coherent and causally earned story |
| `Q12_CHALLENGE` | Fair, final public question with no solution in A or ordinary B |
| `Q13_RECORDS` | Separate B1–B8, append-only ledger and complete B6 |
| `Q14_DERIVATIVES` | No uncommissioned asset and exact stale-source dependency checks |
| `Q15_INTEGRITY` | Every process, persistence, publication and canon claim is evidenced |
| `Q16_BOUNDARIES` | Public, internal and restricted partitions remain isolated |

No gate may be marked `PASS` for a check that was not performed. A source-dependent gate may be `NOT_APPLICABLE` only when it genuinely has no use in the authorized story.

# 11. Phase 8 — disposition and persistence

- `GO`: essential authority and evidence permit drafting.
- `GO_WITH_OMISSIONS`: nonessential missing evidence is named and omitted safely.
- `BLOCKED`: publication or the central story cannot proceed because date, canon, rights, essential source or authority is unresolved.
- `REVIEW_ONLY`: documentary work only; no story or side effects.

A daily run ends at `AWAITING_EDITORIAL_REVIEW` unless an independent action is authorized. No archive commit, canon promotion or release is implied by a successful draft.

For an explicitly authorized write:

1. verify write privilege, target and base revision;
2. compare exact diff against the current head;
3. reconcile any concurrency conflict;
4. separate restricted fields and challenge custody;
5. execute the smallest approved atomic change;
6. verify readback and hash;
7. mark `COMMITTED_VERIFIED` only after proof.

# 12. Revision and duplicate control

For `DAILY_REVISION`, identify the exact existing A revision and accepted editor status. Preserve old A/B revisions; append or supersede only through the actual revision mechanism. Recheck challenged, canon, rights and downstream media dependencies. A duplicate target must be reconciled rather than silently overwritten.

# 13. Fifteen-part B6 transfer

B6 must be independently usable and include:

1. identity and revisions;
2. source and authority basis;
3. production calendar;
4. P/H/F geometry;
5. cast and role spotlight;
6. character internal state;
7. mission and Edubeam state;
8. Koware real-project boundary;
9. story event and scene dependencies;
10. Eternal Dreamfall derivation;
11. research and safety;
12. audience challenge state;
13. visual/media continuity;
14. QA, conflicts and revisions;
15. next-day handoff.

# 14. Final portable directive

On an actual daily commission, retrieve and pin the effective master and companion versions, resolve the real date and role, obtain source snapshots, perform cumulative V1–V6 transformation, create separate A and B, run Q01–Q16 and return an honest process/editorial/persistence/release state. Chronicle #10 creates no automatic episode compilation or title approval.
