## 1. Determination

The dominant production failure was **not that the source chronicle lacked content**. It was that a long-running, globally framed illustration request repeatedly treated each later image as a new *complete mini-chronicle*, causing story resets, title/cover reappearing, new challenges at the end of each collage, wrong quantities/layouts, unfaithful plot resolutions and inconsistent characters. The first 2×2 image has the desired cinematic illustrated storytelling and panel rhythm, but subsequent renders were not bounded to the next **four unique page IDs** nor anchored to the first image. The corrective architecture is **globally plan N pages once, locally render one four-page collage per user continuation, validate, checkpoint and stop**. The v0.09.04 predecessor already required unique IDs and a four-page layout, but its nine-stage pipeline could still be misread as a single uninterrupted whole-comic generation commission. This revision elevates a hard *inter-turn boundary* and a resumable continuity capsule from recommendation to binding render command. No provider compliance is assumed.

The current N=16 job has a full intended map S01[P01–P04], S02[P05–P08], S03[P09–P12], S04[P13–P16]. The user prefers the first collage's **look**; it is not assumed to pass strict portrait aspect or rights QA. No duplicate “final” or challenge is permitted on S01–S03. S04's P16 alone ends in the complete public Challenge A.

## 2. Verified visual observation and an important qualified conflict

The first image visible in this conversation has **1024×1536 actual raster pixels** and a neat 2×2 layout, each cell 512×768. The four cells show: (1) a New Legon HQ establishment and near-send draft, (2) matching transfer-reference evidence but no authorization, (3) contradictory direction signs with a Kuala Lumpur observer-contact image, (4) a discussion separating current site safety from historical archives. Black page dividers, gold narration blocks, white bubbles, lifelike West African faces, a consistent navy title band, office and field visual distinction, and convincing character acting are strong reasons to preserve the reference's creative style and forward continuity. These observations were made from the actual local first image, not from the inaccessible embedded image in the ChatGPT share landing page.

**Aspect conflict:** 1024:1536 reduces to **2:3**, whereas the required 9:16 image would satisfy `16×width=9×height` (and an exact zero-gutter 2×2 sheet of such pages would also be 9:16). Therefore the aesthetically successful first collage does **not** meet the user's new stated strict 9:16 pixel format. It should be marked `LOOK_ACCEPTED_AS_REFERENCE`, `CONTENT_REQUIRES_SOURCE_CHECK`, `ASPECT_FAIL_FOR_RELEASE`. Never falsify geometry, stretch/crop valuable dialogue, erase the liked image, or secretly begin S01 again. Plan eventual same-page aspect repair only with owner permission, or disclose an owner-authorized exception for draft use. The former 16-page collage bundle's upscaled 1080×1920 crops is also not evidence of high-resolution native artwork; multiple underlying graphics were previously rejected by the user for drift and repetition.
