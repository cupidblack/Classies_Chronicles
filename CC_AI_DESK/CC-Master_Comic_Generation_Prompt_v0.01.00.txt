# CLASSIES CHRONICLES

## MASTER COMIC GENERATION PROMPT

**Document Reference:** CC-CGP
**Version:** 0.01.00
**Purpose:** Convert an approved daily chronicle into a sequential illustrated comic.

### 1. OBJECTIVE

Create a high-quality, cinematic comic adaptation of the supplied Classies Chronicles daily chronicle.

The objective is to visually narrate the complete chronicle while preserving its characters, storyline, atmosphere, locations, chronology, dialogue, educational content and established universe.

The comic must be engaging, visually immersive and suitable for mobile-device reading.

### 2. INPUT MATERIAL

The AI will receive:

1. The approved daily chronicle.
2. Relevant character reference images.
3. Character descriptions and aliases.
4. Relevant location references.
5. Existing visual identity guidelines.
6. Previous comic pages where necessary for continuity.
7. The approved audience challenge.

Use the supplied materials as the authoritative references.

Do not invent significant character attributes, relationships, locations or technological capabilities that contradict established canon.

### 3. AUTOMATIC PAGE ALLOCATION

Analyse the supplied chronicle and determine the appropriate number of pages.

Apply the following standard:

* Very short chronicle: 2–3 pages.
* Short chronicle: 3–4 pages.
* Standard chronicle: 5 pages.
* Long chronicle: 6–7 pages.
* Extended or complex chronicle: 8 pages.

Use five pages as the default.

Determine page count according to:

* Number of significant scenes.
* Number of characters.
* Number of locations.
* Dialogue requirements.
* Narrative complexity.
* Important visual developments.
* Educational content.
* Dramatic pacing.
* Audience challenge presentation.

Do not artificially extend a short chronicle.

Do not compress a complex chronicle merely to meet the five-page default.

### 4. PAGE DIMENSIONS

Every individual comic page must maintain:

* Portrait orientation.
* 9:16 aspect ratio.
* Preferred resolution: 1080 × 1920 pixels or higher, where supported.
* High-quality rendering.
* Clear panel separation.
* Mobile-friendly typography.

Each page should ordinarily contain 3–5 readable panels.

Avoid overcrowding.

Where a scene requires additional visual space, allocate another page rather than shrinking the panels.

### 5. NARRATIVE DISTRIBUTION

Distribute the story naturally across the selected pages.

The following is the default five-page arrangement:

PAGE 1 — OPENING AND SETTING

Establish the characters, location, atmosphere and circumstances.

Create an engaging visual opening that immediately introduces the day's narrative.

PAGE 2 — DEVELOPMENT

Depict relevant activities, character interactions, preparations and emerging developments.

Introduce important dialogue and supporting events.

PAGE 3 — PRINCIPAL EVENT

Give the main event, discovery, mission, conflict or significant development appropriate visual attention.

Use cinematic composition and expressive character reactions.

PAGE 4 — CONSEQUENCES AND REFLECTION

Show the consequences of the principal event.

Incorporate relevant educational, spiritual, professional or personal developments naturally.

PAGE 5 — CONCLUSION AND AUDIENCE CHALLENGE

Bring the daily narrative to a meaningful closing point.

Present the audience challenge as the final element.

For shorter or longer chronicles, redistribute these responsibilities across the selected number of pages.

### 6. VISUAL IDENTITY

Maintain a consistent visual identity throughout the comic.

Requirements:

* High-quality cinematic comic illustration.
* Detailed environments.
* Natural character expressions.
* Realistic proportions.
* Consistent clothing and accessories.
* Appropriate lighting.
* Atmospheric backgrounds.
* Clear foreground and background separation.
* Dynamic but understandable composition.
* Consistent colour grading.
* Professional comic-book presentation.

Respect the approved Classies Chronicles visual identity.

Do not arbitrarily change the artistic direction between pages.

### 7. CHARACTER CONTINUITY

Characters must remain visually consistent.

Preserve:

* Facial features.
* Skin tone.
* Hairstyle.
* Age appearance.
* Body proportions.
* Clothing.
* Accessories.
* Established character-specific attributes.

Use supplied reference images wherever available.

A character must not appear significantly different between consecutive pages unless the chronicle explicitly describes a change.

### 8. STORY AND SCENE CONTINUITY

Preserve the chronological sequence of events.

Maintain consistency in:

* Time of day.
* Weather.
* Locations.
* Buildings and interiors.
* Equipment.
* Vehicles.
* Technology.
* Character positions.
* Mission status.
* Clothing.
* Environmental conditions.

Each page must visually connect to the preceding and following pages.

Do not introduce unrelated scenes simply to fill panels.

### 9. DIALOGUE AND TEXT

Use speech bubbles, captions and narration appropriately.

Preserve the supplied dialogue and important narrative information.

Requirements:

* Correct spelling.
* Accurate names and aliases.
* Readable typography.
* Proper speech-bubble positioning.
* Clear distinction between narration and dialogue.
* No invented quotations attributed to real people.
* No unnecessary text duplication.

Where the image-generation system cannot reliably render exact text, reserve clear text areas for subsequent typesetting.

Do not sacrifice visual quality or spelling accuracy to generate decorative lettering.

### 10. EDUCATIONAL CONTENT

Where applicable, incorporate:

* Eternal Dreamfall Bible Study.
* YouVersion Verse of the Day.
* Health & Safety lessons.
* Historical information.
* Scientific discoveries.
* Organisational activities.
* Relevant real-world developments.

Educational content should emerge naturally from the scenes.

Do not turn the comic into a collection of unrelated educational posters.

Preserve the meaning of supplied biblical and educational references.

### 11. AUDIENCE CHALLENGE

The final page must contain the day's audience challenge.

The challenge must:

* Be clearly visible.
* Be easy to read.
* Relate directly to the chronicle.
* Encourage audience participation.
* Be presented as the final narrative element.

Do not reveal the answer.

Do not include hints unless explicitly authorised.

Do not place additional story scenes, promotional material or unrelated text after the challenge.

### 12. COLLAGE GENERATION

Where supported, generate all selected pages as one high-resolution vertical composition.

The composition must contain:

* Clearly separated individual pages.
* Correct sequential order.
* Consistent page proportions.
* Visible page boundaries.
* Appropriate spacing.
* Consistent visual quality.

Each page must internally retain a 9:16 portrait composition.

Do not compress the entire story into one crowded page.

If the image-generation system cannot produce the required tall composition reliably, generate the pages individually.

### 13. FINAL OUTPUT

Produce:

1. One master sequential comic composition, where supported.
2. Individual 9:16 comic pages.
3. A compact page-preview/contact sheet.
4. The page sequence and page descriptions.
5. A note identifying any text requiring final typesetting.

The individual pages must remain suitable for independent mobile viewing.

The final page must end with the approved audience challenge.

### 14. FINAL QUALITY CHECK

Before accepting the comic, verify:

* Correct page count.
* Correct page dimensions.
* Correct reading order.
* Character consistency.
* Location continuity.
* Story accuracy.
* Dialogue accuracy.
* Visual quality.
* Readability.
* Educational accuracy.
* Correct audience challenge.
* No challenge solution disclosed.

The comic must faithfully portray the approved daily chronicle.

Do not introduce major new storyline developments that were not present in the approved written chronicle.

END OF PROMPT.
