## 3. Production pilot exit criteria

An expressly commissioned first N8 comic pilot may be labeled **`COMIC_ASSET_QA_PASSED`** only after an actual permitted A revision, approved character/look rights, real two-sheet/eight-page assets, four independent full pages in **each** measured 2×2 sheet, artistically consistent lifelike/selectively-toony expressions, legible accurate lettering on phone previews, correct approved public challenge terminal placement and every applicable C gate pass have genuine supporting observations. An assembly script's successful pixel crop cannot assert an artistic gate; text-only storyboards can never pass page pixel gates. No approval or publishing action occurs without its separate evidence.
