## 1\. Executive determination

Retain a **three-document CC-03 specialist set**. The master governs what may be produced; the daily suite determines the exact eligible source and accepted state; the comic prompt decides *how to adapt and actually render*; the review proves traceability and records unresolved authority; and QA measures only real documentary/asset evidence. A polished sample visual cannot substitute for a safe, correct, source-locked comic asset. Conversely, prohibiting all cartoon exaggeration would defeat the user's intended mixed expressive art language.

The reviewed examples show a reasonably coherent **cinematic illustrated** family—dark royal/navy blue technology sequences, warm amber book/home scenes, richly shaded believable Ghanaian faces, blue/teal reflective screens, ivory or yellow narration cards, mostly white speech balloons, strong panel dividers, documentary inset and urban establishing shots, with occasional deliberate comic exuberance. They **do not** demonstrate universal typography perfection or uniform geometry. Some examples are dense 2×2 collages with small text; a few have apparent incorrect/mismatched writing or visually incomplete frame space; and exact duplicate content appears under different links. The new style bible preserves what works and treats defects as regression tests, not house style.

The user specifies both a speculative sixteen-page improvement to old five-page work and a **last explicit ordinary standard of eight distinct portrait pages**. This document gives that last specific default precedence **within the newly proposed CC-03** but transparently preserves the upstream CC-UPMP v0.09.01 default of sixteen *until it is actually amended*. Both exact-count overrides are supported. For eight pages, exactly **two** 2×2 portrait sheets; sixteen, exactly **four**; twenty, exactly **five**. No additional duplicate pass is authorized by a request to generate once.

### 3.2 Canonical visual identity distilled from the collection

**Core:** human figures remain realistic, textured and proportionate; not 100% photographic; brush/ink and controlled painterly texture make them unmistakably *comics*. **Emotion:** real expressions are primary, with temporary visibly intentional toony exaggerations at comedic spikes (the rolling-stool surprise is a useful positive example). **Palette:** navy/royal blue and cyan/teal for technical scenes; amber/ivory/gold for story strips, sunlight and reflective passages; warm-to-cool cinematography follows location/time. **Panels:** crisp dividers, compelling close-ups, purposeful urban establishing shots, document/screen detail, thoughtful use of narrator caption cards and speaker-linked white dialogue balloons. **Identity:** prominent but not repeatedly intrusive Classies masthead, no permanent Classies/Koware combined lockup. **Production:** readable 9:16 *single-page originals* first; arrange four full portrait pages in one portrait 2×2 sheet without degrading text size.

**Observed quality risks requiring explicit rejection/repair:** Tiny sentences compressed by source collages; inconsistent apparent name/label spellings across image variants; scene copy potentially implying real routes, approval or status more strongly than facts support; duplicated four-page example art under different repository paths; over-repeated pensive Enka angles; over-filled info tables; occasional visibly unfinished white space (not an authorized page ending); and inconsistent aspect/format among legacy samples. These are **potential observations** from previews, not automated detection reports or evidence that every reference shares them.
