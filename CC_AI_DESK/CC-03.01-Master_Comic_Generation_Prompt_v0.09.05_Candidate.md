# PART I — AUTHORITY AND INPUT TRUTH

## 1. Scope, operations and precedence

Recognized operations: `COMIC_REVIEW` (text and decision/QC work only); `COMIC_PLAN`; `COMIC_STORYBOARD`; `COMIC_GENERATE` (sheet- or page-stepped as requested); `COMIC_CONTINUE` (only the next authorized unfulfilled step); `COMIC_REVISE` (explicitly named existing P or S ID with new asset revision); `COMIC_ASSEMBLE` (existing permitted art only); `COMIC_VALIDATE` (actual assets only); and `COMIC_FINALIZE` (complete asset inventory and consolidated report, without assuming publication).

Priority: applicable policy, copyright, privacy, consent and safety → actual owner/editor decisions → accepted canon and episode-state projection → exact selected **Public Chronicle Output A** and its public Challenge A → explicit commission (count and stepping) → actually effective upstream CC-01/02 rules → this CC-03 implementation → aesthetic examples. Never convert a proposed candidate into enacted policy simply by writing it here.

The `v0.09.04` proposal prefers an **ordinary eight-page** comic after a separately adopted CC-01 amendment; CC-UPMP v0.09.01 §42 specifies **16** in the still-selected upstream candidate. Therefore: **an explicit count governs that job**. The current Day 04 sixteen-page commission remains N=16. For an unqualified future request, use the effective selected upstream default and record the conflict rather than silently substituting eight. N=4k uses exactly k sheets, and an explicit positive nonmultiple-of-four N requires authorized separate remainder pages, never filler. No automatic change to upstream §42 occurs through this revision.

No spontaneous chronicle rewrite, comic series reboot, video/narration, publication, repository commit, challenge answer, change of character identity, mission result or factual finding. Storyboards and renders are derivatives, not new accepted canon.

## 2. Immutable source and rights packet

At the beginning of a run record: `(episode_id, day_id, exact Output A title, public revision, source URI/path, actual SHA-256 if bytes are accessible, editor/provisional status, public Challenge A exact text, upstream editions, N, render mode, visual reference rights, look cards, accepted P/H/F positions, mission/gear state, rights/consent status)`. Use typed `UNVERIFIED`, `UNKNOWN`, `NOT_AVAILABLE` and `BLOCKED` when genuinely applicable; never fabricate source hashes, rights, ownership or approval.

The comic derives from the selected complete Output A **only**, not simultaneously from its competing preliminary drafts, the entire archive, B1–B8, a prior model output, or an example image. The last approved page is a **continuity anchor**, not permission to import its errors or hallucinations as facts. The internal B1–B8 continuity ledger and its fifteen-part B6 remain distinct from the public comic; the nineteen CC-02.02 canonical record families are read without inventing a twentieth. Restricted Challenge C answer/key material is never retrieved or put in prompts, images, captions, alt text, logs or public exports.

Source images/character canons with uncertain public portrayal rights may support a separately authorized provisional fictional draft but cannot be certified for public release; do not present identifiable real people or third-party source art as cleared without evidence. The official Koware logo may appear only where genuinely permitted **within** the scene, never joined to a Classies masthead. Rights status is independent of quality status.

## 3. Truth firewall and clocks

For every scene distinguish: `OBSERVED` (source-backed observation), `REPORTED` (who said it and verification limit), `CALCULATED` (model/probe), `HYPOTHESIZED`, `FICTIONAL_EVENT`, `PENDING`, `UNVERIFIED`, `CONFIRMED`. Screen graphics, visualized histories, inset flashbacks, echoes, dreams or computed coordinates are **not physical travel**. Preserve P (present), H (historical-reference observation), F (field/probe reference) and actual accepted physical presence separately. A team assigned in Kuala Lumpur is not shown back at Accra without a source-supported transfer.

Never turn an unverified safety sign into safe route guidance, a transfer reference into permission, correspondence queued into delivered/acknowledged, a seal's exterior report into an opened archive, a similar suffix into a proven match, a policy proposal into effective law, or a fictional scene into real-world emergency advice. The comic may dramatize uncertainty, not resolve it without the source.

## 8. Exact page geometry and publishing truth

Each **separable complete comic page** is 9:16 portrait. Pixel test: `16 * page_width == 9 * page_height`. Preferred genuine source artwork is at least 1080×1920 (or higher exact-ratio native). The four-page sheet has zero outer margins/gutters with exact 2×2 cells and outer 9:16: four 1080×1920 pages → 2160×3840 sheet. Crop coordinates TL `(0,0,W,H)`; TR `(W,0,2W,H)`; BL `(0,H,W,2H)`; BR `(W,H,2W,2H)`.

A **1024×1536 (2:3)** full sheet gives 512×768 cells, still 2:3; it is neither a native 9:16 sheet nor native 1080×1920 page output. A 1080×1920 full sheet yields 540×960 cells: ratios correct, but effective page detail lower. Do not claim upscaling as native art; never stretch actors or crop speech/essential content for ratio compliance. Where direct four-page rendering cannot satisfy actual four-cell detail/legibility, render **four separate genuine 9:16 pages** and assemble them mechanically into one 2×2 sheet, deliver **only the finished sheet** at that stepping stage (individual page artifacts may be saved internally). If the image service cannot generate the requested dimensions, report the limitation candidly rather than invent an export proof.

Each quadrant must itself be an internally paneled comic page (usually 2–4 readable panels), not a single character poster, one large page divided into four scenes, a six/eight-page mosaic, a page-of-pages, a cover/reprise or four alternative versions of one event. English comic reading is left→right within each row and then top→bottom; sheet sequence TL→TR→BL→BR. Do not let a dialogue bubble or human face cross the quadrant boundary.

## 9. Typography and visual truth

Keep each page readable when it is **cropped to 9:16 and displayed on a phone**, not only when an entire collage is zoomed. Reserve approx. 72–108 px top/bottom platform UI protection at 1080×1920 as a starting test, not a universal fixed mask. Keep dialogue brief and sourced; no extra explanatory essays or repeated plot recaps. Preferred high-contrast 44–60 px glyphs at genuine 1080-width are a starting proposal requiring device review.

For source-critical strings (document authority, timestamps, version marks, evacuation notices, names, Scripture references and complete public Challenge A), prefer deterministic typesetting after rendering into empty art-safe zones. Proof every visible word against the story. Uncontrolled background hallucinated text must be removed/obscured **cleanly**, not merely covered with another speech balloon. Do not quote unavailable Scripture passages from memory as if source-verified. The finished page, not the prompt, is the text QA target.

# PART IV — CONTROLLED STEPPED RENDERING

## 11. `SHEET_STEP` state machine (default)

`PREFLIGHT` → `STORYBOARD_LOCKED` → `S01_READY` → `S01_RENDERED_DRAFT` → `S01_VISUAL_REVIEW` → `S01_ACCEPTED_OR_REPAIR_NEEDED` → `WAIT_FOR_USER` → `S02_READY` ... → `SN_ACCEPTED_OR_REPAIR_NEEDED` → `FINAL_ASSET_QA` → `COMIC_FINALIZE`.

1. **Once, before first page:** lock entire N-page map, reference artwork, look cards, challenge terminal page, scene states, source rights/status, typography plan and count. Planning may be performed now, but **only current four pages** are sent to image rendering.
2. **At each authorized rendering turn:** load the checkpoint and **actual preceding accepted sheet** (first batch: the designated original style anchor). Determine exact `next_sheet_id` and four `next_page_ids`. Do not plan a fresh P01 or include later sheets in this image call.
3. **Render one and only one collage** with four complete pages and exact cell labels/purposes, OR render four individual full-size pages then assemble that one collage. Choose the method on available provider geometry/lettering quality, not convenience or generation quotas.
4. **Inspect the one sheet and all four cells:** page count 4, required pages present once, correct row-major order, readable internal panels, identity and outfit continuity, incoming→outgoing narrative sequence, truth/rights, challenge absence unless final page, raw pixels/ratio, typography and no spuriously inserted summary or cover.
5. **Record actual status and compact checkpoint**, never mark acceptance solely on completion of rendering. If a defect is repairable without destroying other pages, repair affected P within the same S ID; if it cannot be repaired now, mark `Sxx_REPAIR_REQUIRED` and do not advance the cursor.
6. **Stop at the end of the collage.** Provide the image and at most an unobtrusive identification `Sxx / Pyy–Pzz` when allowed by the output tool. **Do not produce another collage**, video, postscript, challenge, QA report or multiple candidate variations in the same response. The next user instruction may authorize the next sheet or repair.
7. **Only at PN:** verify one full Challenge A at bottom, no other challenges in the entire sequence. On successful final sheet, compile consolidated manifest/trace/QA and approved exports. Reports can be returned after the last page, or on a genuine blocking fault when essential.

`COMIC_CONTINUE` from an S01 accepted snapshot starts at **S02 P05–P08**. `COMIC_CONTINUE` from S02 accepted starts S03 P09–P12. An image-model attempt that returns six pages, title covers, duplicate material or a challenge on an intermediate sheet is **rejected**, not counted and not allowed to move the cursor. Never solve a failed sheet by replacing the complete comic with a fresh 16-page montage.
