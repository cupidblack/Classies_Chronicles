## 2. Retain, merge or retire?
**Retain the Master Media Generation Prompt as a specialist.** The CC-UPMP covers audiovisual acceptance gates and broad policy but cannot economically carry platform-specific prompts, motion categories, asset lineage, scene editor timing, audio, clip identity continuity and replenishment intelligence without redundant and unstable content. Retain CC-04.02 as a **review and change-control record**. Add CC-04.03 as media-specific QA/evidence manifest and CC-04.04 as a separately refreshable provider directory. Do not fold provider prices into an adopted CC-UPMP; they expire faster than franchise rules. Preserve v0.04.00 documents unchanged as historical evidence rather than replacing them without owner adoption.

## 7. Regression coverage / evidence needed
**Static document QA:** confirm all CC-04 references exist in package; exact M01–M30 and T01–T30 labels appear once as defined gates/test IDs; 19 ledger family strings match governing ledger; 16 act timecodes cover 00:00–02:40 without gaps; 16:9 dimensions correctly stated; source-code CLI accepts real file paths and never fetches unauthorized URLs; SHA records created only for actual files.

**Tool-level synthetic QA:** valid 4×4 collage slices into sixteen 9:16 cells; wrong geometry fails; strict minimum pixel gate prevents treating thumbnail crops as high-quality pages; independent normalized crop matches expected pixels; staged 1920×1080 frame obeys aspect ratio without stretching; edit output retains center image; metadata records native dimensions and SHA. Test with synthetic images only until actual authorized comic art is supplied.

**Live media pilot (NOT executed in this document review):** approved story locked; real character media and rights verified; provider UI allowance tested; five-second clip generated; character face and clothing stable; camera motion obeys prompt; dialogue quality reviewed; caption timing accurate; no frozen frames, text distortion or watermark violations; full 160/200s (whichever approved) assembled with verified duration and framerate; exact final question visible; accessible audio mix; rights-cleared output archived; owner records approval; published only when instructed.
