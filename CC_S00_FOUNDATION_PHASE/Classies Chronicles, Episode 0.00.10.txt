# Classies Chronicles, Episode 1, The Signal Before Sunrise, begins at 12 sixteen AM. Ghana time, the EduBeam indicator lit up in Amrahia. The calendar system displayed a timestamp: 2167-04-30


T01:13:07Z. The year 2167. No approved probe, no authorization, just a pulse repeating like a quiet heartbeat in the machines. Inside the Yamoah house, the 4 AM alarm sounded. The Bible reading began, and the day started quietly in Adentan. But at Koware Group's Amrahia office, the R&D Lab Specialist was already on the phone. "We need to pause the next calibration," they said. The director replied, "preserve the logs, don't reset anything." At 6:30, commuters lined the road, trotro bells chimed, and life moved as usual across Greater Accra. Except Koware. They knew something was wrong. By 9 AM, the briefing started with unexpected agenda. Instead of ordinary operations, they looked at a timeline linking today, October 28, 2026, to July 2003 and that strange 2167 timestamp. The July 2003 reference felt familiar and safe, but 2167 felt like a door they hadn't opened. The team agreed: no travel until they understood what the signal was. At home that night, the diary said it simply: 'Some discoveries arrive before we are ready to understand them.' Tomorrow, they'll decide whether they're building a time machine or building something that remembers. And that's the first installment, leaving the mystery open for the next working day.
